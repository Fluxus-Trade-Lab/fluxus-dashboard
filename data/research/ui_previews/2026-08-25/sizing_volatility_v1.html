The Percent-Volatility Model

Size by ATR so every position breathes the same

Instead of stop distance, divide the risk budget by the ATR: shares = (equity × vol%) ÷ ATR. Every position then contributes equal daily volatility to the book. Useful when stops vary in quality or when names differ wildly in volatility — a 2% ATR mega-cap and a 15% ATR small-cap stop being sized the same notional is a hidden bet on the wilder one.
