# 盲选卷 B · 收口（目标 B）

> 每个位置 4 个候选，**其中一个是已经存在的版本**（正在用的、或曾经写过的），不标记。只回一个字母。
> 判据是你自己 08-24 定的：**要一读就懂的重话，不要要回味的巧话。**


### S1 · 全篇最后一句（MRNA 长文收口）

**A.** You will get the number I risked before you get the trade. Nobody else hands you that half.

**B.** What I will promise is that every idea here arrives with its size and its stop — the half I control, and the half nobody else publishes.

**C.** Every idea here comes with its size and its stop. That is the half nobody else publishes.

**D.** I can't sell you the next 176%. I can show you what I risked to be there for this one.

**C.** Put your sector next to the index tonight and ask why you still hold the one that's behind.

**D.** Tonight, put your sector next to the index. Then explain the one you're still holding.

### S3 · ② 筛子节收口（人 vs 机器的次序）

**A.** I was already in when the machine spoke up. It votes; it doesn't nominate.

**B.** I bought at noon. The machine caught up at midnight.

**C.** The scanner agreed with me the same night. It has never once gone first.

**D.** The machine confirmed me, it didn't tell me.

### S4 · 「How much」节收口（算术替代纪律）

**A.** I never say no to a bad chart. The formula says it while I'm still admiring it.

**B.** I don't have the discipline to pass on garbage. The arithmetic has it for me.

**C.** A sloppy chart gets a tiny position before I have an opinion about it.

**D.** I never need discipline to pass on garbage. The arithmetic passes for me.

### S5 · 「我砍了什么」节收口（事后看每一刀都错，但还是要砍）

**A.** The gap doesn't ask what you were hoping for.

**B.** I paid for the version of the 19th that didn't happen. That bill comes every time.

**C.** Gaps don't check which direction you were hoping for.

**D.** Every cut looks stupid on the 19th. It looks like rent on the other 19th.

### S6 · 「账户」节收口（确定感的价格）

**A.** Certainty has a price and no calories.

**B.** You can buy the feeling of being sure. You cannot eat it.

**C.** That feeling costs a fortune and is worth nothing.

**D.** Being sure is the most expensive thing on the menu, and it isn't food.

**你选：____**

### S7 · 90 分钟离场那一段的收口（它当天摸到 200，我在睡觉）

**A.** Nearly 200 that afternoon. Time zones are the cheapest discipline I own.

**B.** It ran to 200 that afternoon. I found out the next morning, in bed.

**C.** It touched nearly 200 that afternoon. I was asleep.

**D.** It touched 200 while I was asleep, and I'd take the same nap again.

**你选：____**

### S8 · ③ 图形节收口（为什么是 19 号，没人知道）

**A.** Why the 19th? No idea. I knew where the line was, and I'd seen this shape end the same way too many times to stand somewhere else.

**B.** Why the 19th? Nobody knows. What I knew was that 60 was the line, and I'd watched this shape resolve the same way too many times to ignore.

**C.** I never knew the date. I knew the level, and I knew this shape has a habit.

**D.** Nobody knows why it was the 19th. I only knew where to stand while it wasn't.

**你选：____**

### S9 · 目标从哪来那一段的收口（空上去，空回来）

**A.** Nobody traded it on the way up, so nobody argues on the way back. 120, fast, or I'm wrong.

**B.** Empty up, empty back. Target 120, and quickly, or I'm wrong.

**C.** There is nobody in there to talk price out of going back. 120, and soon, or I'm wrong.

**D.** COVID emptied that whole shelf. Price walks back through an empty room. 120, quickly, or I'm wrong.

**你选：____**

**C.** No chart contains a phase III readout. It contains everyone who will react to one.

**D.** The chart didn't know. It knew who would be selling when it found out.
