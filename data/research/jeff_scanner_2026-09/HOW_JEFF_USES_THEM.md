## 一、四层漏斗（这是核心，比任何单个扫描重要）

原话，2024-02-13（[1757271020007006704](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1757271020007006704)）：

> "1. **master watchlist** of various setups. all screening outputs all go into them. I usually do this
> screen **on a weekly basis**. (if u trade 2 types of setups, u should have 2 master watchlist).
> I have about **200+** in total at the moment.
>
> 2. **stalk watchlist**, a filtered from master watchlist where the stocks in the list are **in the midst
> of setting up within a 5 daily session timeframe**. it's filtered immediately from master watchlist
> **during the weekend**, on week on week basis. I need to emphasis that **I am more than willing for
> names in this watchlist to run without me**, basically because I am keeping true to my own rules and
> do not want randomness in my selection for execution
>
> 3. **focus watchlist**, stocks that are filtered from stalk watchlist and have all price action and
> volume parameters setup for **upcoming immediate session**. I have only **6-7** from earlier session.
> most time I have **less than 12** and I **only set price alerts on them**, solely to focus on this and
> not deviate from the lane of my rules"

加一层，同日（[1757277320677904721](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1757277320677904721)）：

> "for runners in my stall watchlist, I will move them into a **'back watchlist'**. I track the development
> of the outcome in this list **on a weekly basis** and go through a thought process if there's any
> adjustment required to get the winning names into my focus list.
> **most time (80% probbaly) of such stocks usually falter in the breakouts.** eg. broke out from extended
> base **above 4 x ATR%**, loosely traded price action prior to breakouts.
> i feel it is still a good and essential habit to **find growth in your filtering process**."

**数量级**：扫描输出 → master 200+ → stalk（5 日内会成形的）→ focus **6–12**，只对 focus 设价格警报。

## 二、扫描本身：节奏、数量、怎么调

**跑的频率不是每天。** 2024-03-26（[1772522489446543679](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1772522489446543679)）：

> "I utilize a set of **12 swing trading scans** to transfer results into individual watchlists, along with
> **two daily scans** specifically designed to filter setups within all my watchlist. However,
> **most of these scans are executed on a weekly basis due to the repetitive nature of daily results.**"
>
> "Every screen result goes into **their individual watchlist**. eg. 'Strong Movers >10B Mcap',
> 'Strong Movers <10B Mcap', 'Fundamental (CANSLIM)', 'Post Earnings Continuation Base', 'Daily Tightness'.
> I also have watchlist that are already established for 'IPO Base', 'China Top 30' MCap, 'Short Float',
> 'Back Watchlist'."
>
> "…two additional scan parameters: **'Watchlist Scan Price Above/Below EMA Below 5%'** … within a range of
> above and below **5% of the 5-day EMA**. … This is the only watchlist that I will **refresh weekly**
> (delete and rebuild again for the start of the week)."
>
> "**I would review all the stocks in each individual watchlist on each Saturday.**"

**结果条数有硬指标。** 2023-05-20（[1659786288067928064](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1659786288067928064)）：

> "In my opinion, a good precise strong mover scan should **yield less than 60 results**. You want the top
> quality movers. … You can make amendment to include any of your preferred criteria to only
> **streamline the scan, not expand it**."

**太多就换更严的那把，不是放宽。** 同帖对 1-Month Mover：

> "1-Month Mover Exceeding 50% (**I would toggle to this scan instead if there are too many results**
> appearing in '1-Month Mover Exceeding 30%' due to strong market. The whole idea is to look for the
> strongest mover of each time period"

**调参调哪两个。** 2024-06-01（[1796806706028302775](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1796806706028302775)）：

> "Personally the adjustable filter in my settings I would adjust are; **Float, Average Vol** merely to
> **constraint the result to within 20 names**. If you reduce the Price above lowest low 52w of 70%,
> you get **laggards** in your result eg. $FOUR which may not be optimal to focus as a strongest name."

2025-09-06（[1964130023751004326](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1964130023751004326)）：

> "I have **tightened the parameter to avg vol 5M** to keep the results **within 25 liquid names**."

2025-09-01（[1962458102709837975](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1962458102709837975)）ETF：

> "There's **less than 72 results** but i go through almost **150 ETF charts daily**. You can loosen the
> parameters by lowering the 'Volatility Week'"

## 三、扫描不是入场信号 —— 他说了至少四次

> "**screening builds a generic watchlist.** it is stalking the daily and weekly price actions of the
> watchlist that filter to your actionable focus list every week"
> — 2023-07-31（[1685982477561790464](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1685982477561790464)）

> "I need to emphasis this is **not an actionable scan**. If you go through the list and is someone that
> likes to enter on pullback-base reset; **only $OUST $TGTX $TDW $MMYT have price action resetting itself**."
> — 2024-06-01，讲他自己的 CANSLIM 周扫描

> "It is crucial to understand that **many actionable stocks that presents lucrative opportunities often
> emerge a few days or weeks in the scans, rather than on the very day of scanning.** … As I highlighted
> in the trade below, **$YMAB will not appear in a 1 week mover scan on the week it moved.**"
> — 2023-05-20（[1659780808364916737](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1659780808364916737)）

> "some of the extended names have already appear in this weekly scan every week **since as early as weeks
> or even months ago**, so just remain diligent and **monitor the result every week**."
> — 2024-06-01

**这条对我们最重要**：他的扫描是**名字的入库口**，不是**当天的动作**。命中日 ≠ 交易日。

## 四、从 stalk 升进 focus 的判据（他给过一次完整清单）

2023-05-16（[1658343561212067840](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1658343561212067840)），他自己说"above-mentioned
pretty much sums up how I upgrade a stock from my stalk list, into my focus list"：

> "1. high momentum **9% ADR**
> 2. strong RS as evident from the recent move of **$2 to $12 in 6 weeks**
> 3. **vcp with volume dry up**
> 4. **mini bull flag** with price tightening for **6 consecutive session**
> 5. latest **DCR is only 50% of ATR**
> 6. **sitting on rising 10-MA**
> 7. Good liquidity as evident from daily avg vol and $ vol"

**停在 stalk 不升的四个理由**，2023-05-20（[1659795676925145090](https://x.com/jfsrev/status/1659795676925145090)）：

> "i) **Upcoming earnings.** I typically avoid trading stocks with upcoming earnings within the next
> **five days**. However, I am willing to consider trades with an **earnings leeway of six days or more**.
> ii) Price softening, but **remains loose** and seeking more price tightening.
> iii) **10, 20-MA have not yet catch up** the softening price action
> iv) **Running into Declining Major MA (Specifically 200-MA)** … If there's at least a **3xADR% distance**
> in the build up, I will still upgrade them to Focus List.
> iv) **Below 10, 20-MA but trailing it closely.** $APLT … **$1.59 price alert for recapturing above 10-MA**."

**RS 在 setup 之前**（wiki `focus-list.md` 引，2025-05）：

> "**Relative Strength First, Setup Second.**"

## 六、对我们的三条直接含义（我的判断，不是他的话）

1. **他的扫描该进「池」不该进「面板」。** 他自己说是 not actionable、命中日常常不是交易日、
   多数扫描**周频**。我们把它当每日面板会误导——**命中要带「首次入池日」和「已在池中几周」**，
   这两个字段比命中本身有用（对应他的 "appear every week since months ago"）。

2. **结果条数是设计指标，不是副产品。** 他的硬线：mover 扫描 **<60**，调 float / avg vol
   把结果**压到 20–25 个**。我们的面板从来没有「太多就换更严那把」的机制——
   目前是固定阈值 + top 25 截断，截断和收紧不是一回事。

3. **他有一层我们完全没有的：back watchlist。** 跑掉的票单独跟踪、**每周复盘为什么没进 focus**，
   而且他自陈 **80% 会失败**。这是**扫描的校准回路**——我们的 `shortlist_feedback` 只收 Andy 的裁决，
   不收「没选它但它跑了」这一侧。这可能是这次调查里最值钱的一条。
