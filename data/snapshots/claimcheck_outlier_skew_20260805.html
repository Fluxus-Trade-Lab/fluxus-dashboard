Two independent methods agree on DIA to within 0.1–0.3 vol points. In absolute level DIA's calls still trade below ATM and puts above it — the ordinary equity-index shape, not "off the charts".

But that does not refute him. "98th %'ile call skew" is a claim about where skew sits in its own history, and a percentile that high is perfectly compatible with a still-negative level if the normal level is more negative. We do not store a skew history, so the percentile claim is unverified — neither confirmed nor refuted. Building that history is the only way to check it, and it is one series a day.
