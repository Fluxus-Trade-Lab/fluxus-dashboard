What this is not

Not a search for the best parameter. Tuning a threshold until the answer looks good is how a free parameter quietly becomes a conclusion. The sweep only ever reports variance — it never picks a winner, and the published defaults are unchanged by running it.

Failures are kept rather than dropped: a value that makes the computation raise marks the edge of where the method is defined, and skipping it would make the surviving range look wider than it is.
