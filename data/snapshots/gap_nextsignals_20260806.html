The one that matters — Market Profile

Seven of the eight levels in his reference map are named by auction structure, not by options: 8/4 single-print high · 8/3 TPOC · 8/3 VPOC without time diversity · 8/3 period 1–3 area · 8/3 low singles · 7/31 TPOC. We compute none of it.

It needs no new data source — intraday bars from IBKR, which we already pull. TPO letter brackets give TPOC; volume-at-price gives VPOC; the difference between them is precisely what he means by "VPOC without time diversity".

Why it outranks every options feature: his method is two independent frameworks landing on the same price. Our "confluence" today is gamma vs charm — two views of the same dealer book. That is confirming ourselves. His is the auction against the dealer book, which is being confirmed by something else.

The column is headed GEX-VOLUME. He weights by today's volume; we weight by accumulated open interest. Neither is wrong and a strike can be large in one and small in the other — that gap is itself information we currently throw away, and we already fetch the volume.

What costs nothing but discipline

One question per day, asked before the session — "Does the nascent 758.0–760.3 balance hold as acceptance above 8/3's TPOC?" with two named branches and the consequence of each. A fork, not a forecast.
A WHAT CONFLICTS section — he writes down where his own two frameworks disagree and names the rule that breaks the tie. Publishing the disagreement is what makes the agreement mean anything.
Ranking the evidence in one sentence — "Two independent methods landing on 755.6 is the strongest thing in this read."

All three are format. None need data. They can be in tomorrow's brief.
