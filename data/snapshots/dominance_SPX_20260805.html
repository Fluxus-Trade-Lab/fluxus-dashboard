Gamma is delta sensitivity to price; charm is delta sensitivity to time. Gamma says where hedging fires if the market moves, charm where it fires if the market does nothing. A balance that holds is charm-governed; a balance that breaks is gamma-governed.

Is there a handoff?

Closest candidate is 7,900 at |gex| 1.00σ vs |charm| 0.61σ. That is not a draw — a ratio of 1.64 is clearly one-sided. There is no crossover on this grid, and the function says so rather than handing back its least-bad candidate under a name that implies otherwise.

What the numbers are not

The two series have no common unit — dollars of delta per 1% move against dollars of delta drift to expiry. Both normalisations rescale each series against its own spread, so "dominant" always means more extreme for gamma than for charm, each relative to its own grid. It is never a raw magnitude contest, and a different normalisation would move some of these labels.

A strike quiet in both gets neither, not whichever lost the coin flip. A strike measured in only one series is dropped, not awarded.
