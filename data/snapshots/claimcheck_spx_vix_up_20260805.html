4 — mean versus median

At 3–5 sessions out the event sample's median is +0.21% to +0.32% and 57–58% of outcomes are positive, while the mean sits near zero. The mean is being pulled down by a small left tail. "Flat to small negative" is a true statement about the mean and a misleading one about the typical outcome.
