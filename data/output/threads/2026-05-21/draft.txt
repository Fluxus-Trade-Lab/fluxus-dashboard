===THREAD: TECHNICAL===
HOOK: Two reversal patterns showed up across the tape today. Learn to spot them and your entries get a lot cleaner.

1/ Pattern one: the hammer. Long lower wick, small body, followed by a green candle confirming. Sellers tried, failed, buyers took the bar back. TSLA printed a textbook version today.

2/ Pattern two: the three-candle inside-day reversal. Middle candle sets the low. Third candle closes above candle one. It is a shift in control, compressed into 72 hours. CRDO, HD, ORCL all showed it.

3/ ORCL is the cleanest read. Pullback into the demand zone, reversal candle on the retest. Same level that was resistance is now support. The map is doing its job.

4/ Patterns are not predictions. They are bias inputs. Stack them with trend and volume before sizing up. A hammer in a downtrend is noise. A hammer at a demand zone after a breakout is a setup.

5/ Watching for follow-through tomorrow. If the third candle in these combos gets confirmed by a higher close, the reversal is in. If not, it was just a wick.

===END===
