1/ Quarter-end tape found no shortage of catalysts: semis, MU earnings, the 10-year, and Iran oil-deal chatter all collided at once. Rates did most of the damage — ES sat at its 20ema, NQ undercut Thursday's low, and every early bounce got sold.

2/ The breadth worry gets asked every week, so here is the answer: bad breadth does not block a rally by itself. 1999 ran with money piled into a handful of leaders while breadth stayed ugly. Crowded does not mean reversal either — worth watching, not trading around.

3/ Selection framework, in order: sector trend first (right now only semis, software, and biotech qualify), then the stock's own trend, then relative strength and distance from the 52-week high, then leadership — broken out yet, tight-stop entry, how it acted on the left side.

4/ Rotation is happening under the surface while indices chop. Rate-sensitive names like ITB and XLU are bottoming, same setup as CAT and GEV — though bottoming is not reversal. Traditional semis ASML and TSM held up better than the AI-adjacent names catching the pullback.

5/ Earnings risk management, live: total MU exposure capped at 10% of capital, concentrated in DELL and SOXL only, stops honored either way. A position sized that way can be down 20% and still not do real damage. That is what sizing is for.

6/ On the board into next week: pullback names AMD, INTC, ARM, TEM, NBIS; breakout candidates STX, GH, HNGE, MRVL. ARM broke under 290, INTC showing the weaker relative strength in the group — both flagged for continued selling pressure into the gap.

7/ Macro overhang: Iran agreeing to suspend uranium enrichment for sanctions relief could be the deal that sticks. March saw the same setup collapse when it could not get closed. If this holds, expect relief across the board, not just oil. IWM's high near 282 confirms risk-on.

8/ Coaching note: a stop order is a loss you already accepted in your head the moment you placed it, confirmed or not. If pulling the trigger feels hard, the position is either sized too large, or you have gotten married to being right.
