5/ Narrow leadership can carry a tape for a while. It cannot carry it forever — eventually everyone is crowded into the same five names and there is no one left to buy. Healthy markets grow new leaders. This one has not, yet.

6/ Afternoon flip: IWM went red to green, TLT/IWM/DIA all undercut premarket lows and bounced — the capitulation-then-reversal shape that tends to mark a short-term low. Trapped shorts are fuel. Calling it, carefully, a decent start to October.

7/ Coaching note: an hourly-level bull trap resolves with an hourly-level bottom, not a daily one — match the timeframe of the trap to the timeframe of the reversal. For sizing, 30-minute bars are plenty. Anything finer than that is risk management, not signal.
