HOOK: Sunday Globex handed us a textbook trend test. Here is how to read the tape when the market comes back to touch its own line in the sand.

1/ ES Sunday Globex session. Hourly chart retested the blue cloud on the left side — same level as the 5EMA on the daily. Trend structure still intact.

2/ Why this level matters. Daily 5EMA is where short-term momentum lives. When price pulls back to it in an uptrend, two things happen at once. Shorts cover into the retest. Buyers step in to defend the trend.

3/ The read is simple. Hold the 5EMA and the map stays bullish. Lose it decisively and the tape tells you something has shifted. Everything else is noise until that line breaks.

4/ Watching how ES opens the week from here. A clean bounce off the retest confirms buyers are still in control. A close below flips the read. Levels do the talking.
