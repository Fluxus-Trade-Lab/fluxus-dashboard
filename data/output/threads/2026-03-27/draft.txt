===THREAD: MACRO===
HOOK: Energy has been the last man standing — and the mechanics of next week may be the moment it finally cracks.

1/ Oil has led since January. Memory faded. Optics faded. Energy is now the last sector standing above all moving averages. That is worth tracking — and also means energy is next in line when the cycle turns.

2/ There is a structural headwind most equity traders are ignoring: as long as crude trends up, rates stay elevated. Rates up means equity upside is a fight, not a gift. The setup is not clean for longs here.

3/ The news cycle has a pattern. Escalatory headlines and military developments hit off-hours. Headlines designed to suppress oil appear during active sessions, near technically sensitive levels. Trump is watching the tape. The put is real — it is just much lower than most people expect.

4/ Pension fund month-end rebalancing hits early next week. It is large. Likely effect: profit-taking in energy, a mechanical pop in beaten-down names. That move up is not signal. It is plumbing.

5/ If the EOM pump reaches the 5ema or 10ema on indexes, that is the level to short — a rebalancing artifact, not a trend reversal. Nuclear and solar are the exceptions: two sectors holding up with a structural story that outlasts this correction.
