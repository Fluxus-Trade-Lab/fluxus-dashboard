3/ ARKK is the short focus as the rate hike narrative builds. When headline risk fades and the market reprices policy again, high-multiple growth is the first to move. ARKK leads that unwind.

4/ Energy names — XLE, XLU — are the last pocket of real strength. Everything else has sold off. High-beta themes like optics and space are on watch if ES breaks and holds below 6475.

5/ This week's base case: range-bound chop into a final April flush. A 2-3 day short squeeze is possible. A drop is also possible. Best plan: sit on hands until the market reaches an extreme level.

3/ Crude oil slipped back below 90. Gold holds above 4300. Together they say the market is still pricing elevated risk — not in relief mode, despite the bounce. Watch these two as leading tells.

4/ Bollinger Bands are tightening on the index. That compression resolves in one direction. Until it does, this is a range-bound tape. Trading a range like a trend is the fastest way to get chopped up.

5/ If this bounce is the start of something real, a Follow-Through Day is required — a volume-confirmed surge after the initial rally attempt. Without that confirmation, every bounce stays a counter-trend move until proven otherwise.

===THREAD: RISK===
HOOK: Knowing what to buy is only half the trade. Knowing when to enter, how large to size, and when to stop is the other half.

1/ The time to short was into declining moving averages — not into today's tape. If you missed that entry, the trade is gone. Chasing weakness into a potential undercut low is how small losses become large ones.

2/ Undercut and rally is the right framework for longs right now. Let the market make a new low, watch for the reversal, then enter with a defined stop. That setup is cleaner than buying into a falling tape.

3/ Defense days exist for a reason. If today's up move gets fully negated, the read turns more bearish. On a day this news-driven, adding new swing exposure means taking on risk without a corresponding edge.

4/ On speculative names, size is everything. Capping a name like SATL at 1.5% of capital means you can hold through volatility without getting stopped out before the thesis plays out. The position size determines how long you stay in the trade.

5/ Once a name closes above a key level, the plan turns mechanical: add to the position or trail the stop. Decide the rule before the close. Reacting in real time to a moving price leads to inconsistent execution every time.

===END===
