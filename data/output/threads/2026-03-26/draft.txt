===THREAD: MACRO===
HOOK: Tech is in expansion — not a dead-cat bounce — and the next AI bottleneck is already forming.

1/ The compression in tech is done. MRVL, AMD, ARM are entering expansion phase. This is not a relief rally — it is the next leg. Expansion after compression is a different kind of setup.

2/ The current AI bottleneck is CPU. Compute rental prices are rising. The demand is real and measurable. The question worth asking now: when agent AI scales, what becomes the next constraint?

3/ Agent AI, optics, and memory remain Tier 1 themes. Not story stocks — these are where hardware demand is being pulled forward structurally. They lead. Watch them to read the tape.

4/ China is a special case as always. A potential bottom is forming. Not a high-conviction long yet — but relative strength here while the US tape is uncertain is worth tracking.

5/ Agriculture and coal continue to be the low-index-correlation play. If there is one more downside leg in the broad market, these should hold. Diversification that actually works.

===THREAD: SETUPS===
HOOK: The watchlist is tiered — each tier has different entry conditions. Know which tier you are trading before you size in.

1/ Tier 1 unchanged: Agent AI, optics, memory. These set the tone. When they lead, the rest of the tech tape has room. Track them first — they are your early warning system.

2/ Tier 2 has the cleanest near-term setups. ARM is a Day 2 low-risk long. AMD is a touch weaker but Day 2 remains in play. MRVL is slow and steady — best entry is a reclaim of PDH.

3/ On MRVL specifically: do not force the entry. The cleanest trade is a clean move back above prior day high. That is your confirmation. Patience here pays better than chasing the open.

4/ Tier 3 names — KLIC, NBIS, IOT, FIGS, SOLS, YOU, TEL, WULF — all require a follow-through day first. Do not front-run. Let the tape prove itself before committing exposure.

5/ Tier 4 — CAT, NXT, SEDG, ALB — better expressed through options than shares. Cleaner risk definition, less capital at risk in names where the move may be slower or choppier.

===END===

===THREAD: RISK===
HOOK: One rule above all others this week — no profit cushion means no new trades.

1/ If you are in the red this week, the answer is not to trade your way back. That is how a manageable loss becomes a problem. Only add positions if you are sitting on realized cushion.

2/ Risk less is not a cliche — it is the principle that keeps you in the game long enough for the edge to play out. Sub-40% win rate means the losses will come. Manage them or they manage you.

3/ Agriculture and coal via ETFs are real opportunities — CORN ETF, Wheat ETF — but liquidity is thinner than tech. May and June options are cleaner than direct ETF exposure for most sizes.

4/ Even if there is one more downside leg in the broad market, these low-correlation plays should show relative strength. The hedge is structural, not a guess. Size the position to reflect that.

===END===
