===THREAD: MACRO===
HOOK: Multiple signals converged on March 19 as a potential correction low — here is the confluence checklist and why it still does not mean the coast is clear.

1/ You do not call exact bottoms. You look for confluences that reduce downside risk on a long entry. On 3/19, several arrived at once: extreme fear readings, SPX battling its 200-day SMA, DXY showing a top, VIX rejection, and a DeMark 13 count low.

2/ Quad-witching expiration week adds another layer. Markets tend to find direction after the pin resolves. Friday is about mechanics — Monday is when you get your real answer.

3/ The geopolitical narrative is shifting. War headlines are moving from escalation toward negotiation. That alone will not drive a sustained rally, but it removes a headwind that has been weighing on sentiment for weeks.

4/ That said: this bounce needs more than a sentiment shift. Index structure requires real follow-through — not just a relief trade. Watch for a failed breakdown of the November low. That would be the more significant tell.

5/ Index weakness likely continues into next week and possibly through April. Even with all these signals, the index is heavy. Do not confuse confluence with certainty. Define your risk and watch Monday.

===END===

===THREAD: TECHNICAL===
HOOK: The MOVE index flagged risk before VIX did — and one Nomura level on SPX turned out to matter more than any chart pattern.

1/ Most traders watch VIX for volatility signals. The sharper tell today was the MOVE index — Treasury volatility. It tends to lead equity volatility. When it spikes, pay attention before the equity market catches up.

2/ Short-end yields spiked unusually today. Movement in the front of the yield curve is not normal market noise. It amplifies the MOVE signal and adds a structural layer of caution that VIX alone would not show you.

3/ On SPX, the key levels: 6508, 6500, 6493, 6488, 6480. Resistance stacked overhead. The most important one today: 6475 — flagged by Nomura as a major institutional reference. That is exactly where buyers stepped in.

4/ Friday was an opex pin day. Price mechanics dominate on expiration — intraday direction is often misleading and mean-reverts into the close. The real signal comes Monday when the pin dissolves and genuine flow takes over.

===END===

===THREAD: SENTIMENT===
HOOK: When everyone knows the trade, the trade is already over — what VG and LNG teach about news flow and consensus.

1/ A catalyst gives you an entry. But when the move has already started two days before the headline hits consensus, you are no longer early — you are the exit liquidity for those who were.

2/ How news-driven trades decay: first movers enter ahead of the crowd, capture the cleanest move. Consensus arrives, the trade becomes crowded. First movers take profits into strength. A second thrust follows — but it is messier, more volatile, less clean upside.

3/ Qatar's supply disruption gives LNG structural upside in the mid-to-long term. The trade thesis is real. But the optimal entry window was two days ago. From here, you trade the chart — not the headline.

4/ In a weak index environment, only the strongest names deserve your attention. Laggards that have not moved yet are not coiled springs — they are warning signs. Relative strength is the filter. Everything else waits.

===END===

===THREAD: RISK===
HOOK: On days like this, risk management is the trade — not the setups.

1/ On a heavy opex Friday with a weak index, the most important decision is what not to do. No new longs. No chasing. The index is heavy, but your existing positions do not require you to add risk.

2/ If you are in positions, zoom out. The 30-minute or 1-hour chart is your frame — not the 5-minute. Intraday noise on expiration day will shake you out of good trades if you let it pull your attention down.

3/ On hedging: shorting the index directly here is reckless. The better hedge is the weakest instrument in your universe. ARKK should underperform in any environment — that is a more precise hedge than an outright SPX short.

4/ Keep powder dry. Having cash and defined risk on hedges means you can act with conviction if a larger breakdown develops — instead of scrambling to manage damage when it counts most.

5/ The MOVE index is the one risk signal worth watching into next week. Bond volatility has been the leading indicator all session. As long as it stays contained, the crash risk is at caution level — not panic level.

===END===
