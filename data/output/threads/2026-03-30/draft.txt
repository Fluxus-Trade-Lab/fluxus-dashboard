===THREAD: MACRO===
HOOK: Three weeks, same pattern. Strong Monday through Wednesday, puke Thursday-Friday. Here is why that is not going to stop.

1/ Three weeks running: SPX rallies early in the week, fades into Friday. Nobody wants to hold through the weekend when headline risk can reset the tape overnight. This is not random — it is a behavior pattern with a cause.

2/ When past growth leaders get taken out — not just correcting, but failing — that is distribution. Institutions lightening up. A real bottom does not look like this. This is a crowded unwind working its way through.

3/ Druckenmiller said "AI trade is over" weeks before the latest geopolitical escalation. He may have been early. Early is not wrong. The trade that drove 2023-2024 is working through its hangover.

4/ One scenario that changes the tape: peace deal plus Mag7 earnings at end of April. That combination is a legitimate catalyst. Not betting on it yet. But it is on the calendar.

5/ EOM rebalance flows are real. The more red going into Thursday, the more violent the bounce on Friday. Trade it as mean reversion, not a regime change.

===THREAD: RISK===
HOOK: Institutions had one goal today: deleverage. A lot of price movement happened. Almost none of it was accumulation.

1/ Today was a deleveraging session. Winners taken off, shorts covered on the beaten-down names. That creates action in both directions but says nothing about the trend. Do not confuse volume with conviction.

2/ The plan going in was simple. If SPX holds 6400 at open, trim shorts, add ES long, target 1%. Define the scenario before the open, execute it, leave it alone. Small bandwidth day means small plan.

3/ Trimming BE and PL early turned out to be right. When the index pump faded and chips did not follow, sitting light was the correct position. The setup looked right — the timing was not. That is a different kind of wrong.

4/ One trade can reset an entire month's equity curve. It does not need to be the most complex setup. It needs to be sized correctly and held with a plan. That is the edge — not the win rate, not the percentage.

5/ Leaders become trash in days, not weeks. AXTI, LITE, PLTR, ASTS — all failed this week. The lesson is not to avoid leaders. Keep stops tight when they start breaking and do not add to fading strength.
