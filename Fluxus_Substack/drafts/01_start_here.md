# Start Here — What This Letter Is

*Section: —  ·  Audience: everyone  ·  Day 1*
*⚠️ 一稿。标题和开场句需过七道闸,备选见文末。*

---

**What this letter is**

A letter about how much. Every idea here arrives with its entry, its stop, and what I'm risking in percent — of the trade and of the account.

A record kept in public, losers included. My win rate is 39.9%. You will see the ones that didn't work, because a letter that only shows winners teaches the wrong job.

A method you can run without me. I measure. You decide.

**What this letter is not**

Not alerts. Nothing here is timed for you to copy. I post my own trades because a method you can't see me actually run is just a claim.

Not predictions. I don't have opinions about where the market goes. I have measurements of what it's doing and what I'm risking while it does it.

Not advice, and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. I don't know your account, your taxes, or how well you sleep. Measure your own water.

---

The market is a game. Most people play it with their feelings.

I'm a full-time swing trader in Japan. I started as a day trader, got humbled in the way that everybody gets humbled, and grew into swinging leaders when momentum is loud and trading ES when it isn't. I learned under a hedge fund manager who had been through every bear market since the 1980s, which is a long way of saying I learned defense before I learned anything fun.

The thing I keep coming back to is that the market isn't the world. It takes real-world inputs and then mostly ignores them. The crowd plays on feelings and arrives late. The big funds play on autopilot and sell whatever their risk limits tell them to sell, which makes them the calmest and most reliable counterparty you will ever get. My only edge is being the person in the room who did the arithmetic before he felt anything.

So there are no calls here. There is no price target pulled out of the air, and there is nothing you're supposed to buy in the next four minutes. What there is, on every single idea, is a number for how much.

That's the gap I kept noticing. Everyone tells you what to buy. Almost nobody tells you how much — and how much is the part that decides your year. You can be right about the name and still finish flat because you sized it like a lottery ticket, or right twice and give it all back on the third because nothing told you where to stop. I have been all of those people.

I won't promise to be right. Accuracy isn't mine to control and anybody who promises it is selling you something. What I'll promise is the part I do control: the thinking always walks the last step. You will never finish one of these and have to ask me how much.

**What you'll get**

**Size & Stop** is the flagship, two or three times a week. Ideas with their entry, their stop, and their risk in percent.

**The Weather Report** goes out Mondays and it's free. Market regime, breadth, dealer gamma — measured, not forecast. It's the weather, not a command.

**The Ledger** is monthly. The whole book opened up: what I made, what I lost, the three worst trades of the month and what each one changed about the method. If a month is bad you'll read about it in detail.

The first few pieces below are the method and the record. Read those before you decide whether to pay me anything.
