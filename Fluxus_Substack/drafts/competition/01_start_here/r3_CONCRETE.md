# 01_start_here · R3 修辞轮:抽象 → 可数物(10 处)

*为什么是这一轮:08-25 我给 About 写的稿被 Andy 判「一堆很空的话,没有实质」。*
*病根是**读了对标的结构摘要就用自己的抽象词填格子**。而 JB / TSF 的 About 里
**每一条都是数字或专名,整份清单没有一句抽象**。*
*本轮把这条标准倒回来,逐处扫 01 的正文。*

**判据**:① 具体压过抽象 ② 可核对 ③ 不说教 ④ 有画面 ⑤ 省字

---

**V21 · 「The market is a game. Most people play it with their feelings.」**

> 抽象。而且"多数人凭感觉"是一句谁都能写的断言。

改:
> The market takes real-world inputs and then mostly ignores them. That's not cynicism, it's the only reason a measurement is worth more than an opinion here.

---

**V22 · 「got humbled in the way that everybody gets humbled」**

> "以所有人都被教做人的方式被教做人" —— 一个字的信息量都没有。

改:
> I started as a day trader and spent two years learning that being right at 10:04 and wrong at 10:11 pays the same as being wrong twice.

---

**V23 · 「The crowd plays on feelings and arrives late.」**

> 保留后半("The big funds play on autopilot and sell whatever their risk limits tell them to")—— 那半句是具体的机制。前半是断言。

改:
> The crowd shows up after the move is on the chart. The big funds don't show up at all — they sell whatever their risk limits tell them to sell, on a schedule, without an opinion. That makes them the calmest counterparty you will ever get.

---

**V24 · 「the thinking always walks the last step」**

> 🔴 **这是全文最空的一句**,而且它正是我被批评的那种句子 —— 漂亮、要回味、说了等于没说。

改:
> What I control is that the arithmetic is finished before I click, and that you can see it afterwards.

---

**V25 · 「A method you can run without me. I measure. You decide.」**

> 三个短句听起来很硬,但"你能不靠我运行"是承诺不是证据。

改:
> Every number here is one you can recompute. The risk per idea is 0.25%. The size is that number divided by the distance to the stop. There is nothing in the formula that requires me.

---

**V26 · 「how much is the part that decides your year」**

> 载重句,不能删,但可以**给它一个可数的支点**。

改(接在原句后,不替换):
> …and how much is the part that decides your year. My risk on a single idea is 0.25% of the account, fixed before I click. That one number, and where I put the stop, sets everything else.
