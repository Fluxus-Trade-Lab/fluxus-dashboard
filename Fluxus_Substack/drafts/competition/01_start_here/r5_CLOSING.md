**判据**:① 只留一个 takeaway ② 不说教 ③ **非格言体** ④ 冷正文 / 暖 P.S. 的温差 ⑤ 指向下一个动作

> The reason I show the losses isn't modesty. It's that a 39.9% win rate is the actual shape of this job, and if I hid that from you, the first six red trades in a row would make you think you'd broken something. You wouldn't have. That's just Tuesday.

**V42 · 换成具体的连亏长度**
> …the first six red trades in a row would make you think you'd broken something. Six in a row happened to me twice this year. Nothing was broken either time.

---

**V43 · 收在读者的下一个动作**
> Before your next position, write down the price at which you'd admit you were wrong. Then divide. That's the whole letter, and it's the only part of my job you can copy.

---

**V44 · 收在 47 笔**

**V46 · 认错收口**

**V47 · 台账收口**

## 淘汰

- **V43 下一个动作**:⑤ 满分但 ② 只有 2 —— **`Before your next position, write down…` 是命令句**,
  而这封信全篇没有一句在教读者做事。收口处破这个规矩,前面 800 字的克制就白攒了。
  *(这条和 MRNA 那轮淘汰 v45「问题收」是同一个判断。)*
- **V44 收在 47 笔 / V49 一句收口**:都对,但**都在重复正文已经说完的话**,收口不能只是复述。
- **V45 反向承诺**:MRNA 那篇的冠军装置,但**那篇是复盘,这篇是入口**。
  入口处承诺"每个想法都带仓位和止损"⚠️ **正撞 `09_PRODUCT_DEFINITION` 的矛盾 1** ——
  在两档分界线定案前,这句不能作为 Substack 的收口承诺。
