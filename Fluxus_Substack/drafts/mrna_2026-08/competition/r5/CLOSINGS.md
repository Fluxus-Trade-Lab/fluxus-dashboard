# Round 5 — 收口竞赛（10 版）

*同样的事实、同样用词标准，十种收法。收口决定读者带走什么，也决定他收不收藏。*

**判据**：① 只留一个 takeaway ② 不说教 ③ Andy 用词六条 ④ 和刊名（How Much）咬合 ⑤ 不重复正文已说过的话

---

**v41 · 三层（v5 现状）**
> **Stop asking do I like it. Ask how close can my stop go.** The first question decides your mood. The second decides your size.
>
> The news is never in the chart. **The price of being there is, and that price is denominated in pain.**

---

**v42 · 单句收**
> The news is never in the chart. **The price of being there is.**

---

**v43 · 台账收**
> `$MRNA · in 62.72 · stop 60 · risk 0.233% · out +23R`
>
> That line went up before the trade resolved. Every one of them does. **That is the only promise I make, and it is the only one I can keep.**

---

**v44 · 回到运气**
> **0.25% is what I paid to be in the room when the luck walked in.** I cannot make the luck show up. I can decide, in advance and in writing, what it costs me to be standing there when it does.

---

**v45 · 问题收**
> Before your next position, do not ask whether you like it. **Ask how close your stop can go, then divide.** That is the entire letter, and it is the only part of this trade you can copy.

---

**v46 · 我在睡觉收**（v39 移到这里）
> It touched nearly 200 that afternoon and I was asleep, six hours flat by then. **I did not catch the top and I was never trying to** — I was trying to be in the room with a stop I could afford, which is a much smaller claim and a much more repeatable one.

---

**v47 · 疼收**
> 0.25% is not a risk parameter. It is how much this one trade was allowed to hurt, and I set that number on a calm Thursday morning before I knew anything. **Bound the pain first. The rest of it is arithmetic you can do on a napkin.**

---

**v48 · 反向承诺**
> I will not promise you the next 176%. Nobody can, and the ones who do are selling something. **What I will promise is that every idea here arrives with its size and its stop**, which is the half I control and the half nobody else publishes.

---

**v49 · 双句收**
> **Position size is not risk. Stop distance is risk.**
>
> The news is never in the chart. **The price of being there is.**

---

**v50 · 台账 + 疼（混合）**
> **0.25% is what I paid to be in the room when the luck walked in** — and 0.25% is not a risk parameter, it is how much this trade was allowed to hurt. I set it on a calm Thursday before I knew anything at all.
>
> The news is never in the chart. **The price of being there is, and that price is denominated in pain.**
>
> `$MRNA · in 62.72 Aug 14 · stop 60 · risk 0.233% · out Aug 19 · +23R`
