## 判据 = Andy 亲写的三句

不是抽象标准，是三个可比对的样本：

1. *You will leave with three things: **Sector strength picks which one, tightness filters stocks to trade and risk determines position size.***
2. *I never ask will it go up, **I only try to hunt the next big elephant trade with tight risks**.*
3. *Sector RS isn't sentiment — **it's the footprint of Institutional algo monkeys**.*

### 从这三句里读出的用词规则

| # | 规则 | 他怎么写 | 我怎么写错的 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **实动词，不要抽象名词化** | picks / filters / determines / hunt | *becomes division instead of a feeling* |
| **2** | **交易台行话，不要文学比喻** | elephant trade · algo monkeys · tight risks | *the footprint of machines that don't have any* |
| **3** | **第一人称现在惯常时** | I never ask · I only try to hunt | *All three are knowable before I click*（无人称） |
| **4** | **不要格言式收尾** | 三句全部平收 | *Best trade I ever made.* / *The rest is plumbing.* |
| **5** | **不要 how…how…how 排比脚手架** | 直接三个主谓宾并列 | *how X, how Y, and how Z* |
| **6** | **一句说完，不铺四句** | 一行解决 algo monkeys | 我写了四句解释机构为什么被迫 |

**v23 · 零比喻**｜删掉所有形象，只留动作和数字

**v24 · 现在惯常时**｜通篇 I do / I never / I only

**v25 · 身体动词**｜hunt / stand / carry / bleed / walk

**v26 · 冷器械**｜measurement 语汇

**v27 · 三句的延长线**｜把他三句的织理原样往下织

**v28 · 禁作家动作**｜无破折号揭示、无三段排比、无金句

The group had negative three-month excess every single session that month, and software had the best in the market. **Sector RS isn't sentiment — it's the footprint of Institutional algo monkeys.** I was in the wrong group and the data had said so for two weeks.

**v29 · 口语直录**｜像在 Discord 里打字

**v30 · 混合最优**｜取 v21 的词 + v27 的织理 + v28 的克制
> **I never ask will it go up**, I ask who is being bought — and through the first half of August the answer was not semis, **which I owned and kept owning while they didn't work.** $MU ran 4% on the 17th and I felt smart for a day; on the 18th it took all of it back. The group hadn't printed a green number all month. **Sector RS isn't sentiment — it's the footprint of Institutional algo monkeys**, and mine had been walking out for two weeks while I watched.
