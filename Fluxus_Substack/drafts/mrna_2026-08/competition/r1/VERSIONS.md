# Round 1 — 10 版

*每版 = 开头（判断语域的地方）+ 结构招式 + 收口。R1 只比"假设"，胜出者在 R2 展开全长。*
*事实层锁死；Andy 三句必须保留其意。*

---

## v01 · 基线（Andy register）
**轴**：整句流动 + 角色词。= `DRAFT_v4_trunk_en.md` 全文。

> **Aug 14.** I bought $MRNA at 62.72, stop at 60, five percent of the account.
> **Aug 18.** The market had been deteriorating for three sessions and the position had gone nowhere for three days, so I cut half, flat.
> **Aug 19, pre-market.** The phase III data hit.
> **Aug 19.** It opened up 84% and closed up 176.97%. I was out ninety minutes after the open.
>
> Those are the facts. Now the review, and the review has one point: **in the twelve days before that headline, nothing I did right had anything to do with it.**

**收口**：*The news is never in the chart. The price of being there is. And that price is denominated in pain.*

## v02 · 账本体
**轴**：每一节都从一行台账开门，散文是台账的注脚。

> `$MRNA · in 62.72 · stop 60 · 5% · risk 0.217% · out +23R`
>
> That line is the whole trade, and everything below it is me explaining how the line got written. I publish one of these before every position resolves, which is the only reason you should believe any of what follows.
>
> The part people ask about is the middle number. Not the entry, not the exit — the 0.217%. **I never ask "will it go up." I only hunt for the next big elephant trade with tight risk**, and the risk is the only half of that sentence I control.

**结构**：三个筛子各自开一行台账 → `SECTOR · software +0.216 · semis −0.118 · verdict: not semis` / `SURFACING · 5/5 · self-pct 100` / `CHART · band 4.1% · stop 0.73 ATR`
**收口**：*Every trade gets a line. This one happened to be worth writing about.*

## v03 · 第二人称
**轴**：全程对"你"说，我的交易只是例证。

> You are going to see a stock go up 176% in a day, and you are going to want to know how somebody was already in it. The answer is boring, and it happened twelve days before the news, and none of it required knowing anything about oncology.
>
> Here is what you would have needed. **Sector strength picks which one, tightness filters the trade, and risk determines the size.** That is the entire method, and I am going to show you all three on one position I took on August 14.

**结构**：每节以「你今晚能做的」开门，我的动作当证据。
**收口**：*You do not need my trade. You need the three questions, and you need to answer them before the chart shows up, not after.*

## v04 · 纯时间轴
**轴**：取消方法节，方法全部嵌进日子里。十二天，十二个小节。

> **July 31 — earnings, and I am not watching.** MRNA reports and goes nowhere for five sessions. I own software and semis. Biotech is not on my screen.
>
> **August 7 — the day the character changes.** It engulfs the 6th, up 10%, and takes back the 21 EMA. What matters is not the 10%, it is that price never goes below that bar again. A range is born, and ranges are where I start paying attention.
>
> **August 10 — it takes the 50-day back.** Still not my problem. Semis are my problem.

**结构**：7/31 · 8/7 · 8/10 · 8/12 · 8/13 · 8/14 · 8/15–18 · 8/19 pre · 8/19 open · 8/19 +90min · 8/19 after-hours · the account
**收口**：*Twelve days, eleven of them boring.*

## v05 · 会员问答
**轴**：用会员真实问的问题当小标题（和宣传推文的"members asked"完全咬合）。

> Some members asked how I was already in $MRNA the morning it went up 176%. The honest answer is that I bought it twelve days earlier for reasons that had nothing to do with the news, so instead of answering in the chat five times I am going to answer it properly here.
>
> ### "Why were you even looking at biotech?"
>
> I was not. I was watching $MU, **because I owned semis and they were not working.**

**结构**：小标题全是问句 —— *Why biotech? · How did you know it was tight enough? · Why only 5%? · Why did you cut before the news? · Why did you sell at 150 and not 200?*
**收口**：*If your question is not in here, it is in the Discord. That is where these came from.*

## v06 · 认错优先
**轴**：从 MU 那笔亏损开门，赢的那笔到很后面才出现。

> The trade I want to talk about first is the one that lost money. On August 17 I was holding semis and $MU ran 4% and I felt clever for about a day. On the 18th it gave everything back and then some, and by the close I was looking at a group that had not printed a single positive number all month.
>
> That is where the $MRNA trade actually starts — not with a chart I liked, but with a chart I owned that was not working.

**结构**：MU 亏 → 板块 RS 是对账单不是观点 → 轮动 → 才引出 MRNA
**收口**：*The 176% is the part you clicked for. The $MU is the part that made it possible.*

## v07 · 极短
**轴**：1,200 词硬上限。只留三个筛子 + 多大 + 账，叙事压到骨头。

> I bought $MRNA at 62.72 on August 14, stopped at 60, five percent. Twelve days later it was up 176% in a session and I was flat ninety minutes after the open.
>
> Nothing I did right had anything to do with the news.
>
> **Sector strength picks which one, tightness filters the trade, and risk determines the size.** Three filters, in that order, and the first one ran before this stock existed to me.

**结构**：三节，每节 300 词，全部清单化。零叙事。
**收口**：*Position size is not risk. Stop distance is risk. That is the whole letter.*

## v08 · 教学优先
**轴**：规则先立，交易当例证。像教材而非复盘。

> There are three questions between a chart and a position, and most people only ask the first one.
>
> **Which one.** **How tight.** **How much.**
>
> I am going to answer all three with numbers from one trade — $MRNA, August 14, which happened to go up 176% five days later. The 176% is not the lesson. It is just what made the lesson worth reading.

**结构**：Q1 Which one（板块 RS）→ Q2 How tight（束宽）→ Q3 How much（0.25%）→ 每节末尾"the trade said"用 MRNA 兑现
**收口**：*Three questions. The market only ever tests the third one.*

## v09 · 书信体
**轴**：写给一个具体的人（刚开始做这行的读者），全程"you and I"。

> You wrote asking how anybody is already inside a stock that goes up 176% before the news, and whether it is luck. Half of it is. I want to show you which half, because the other half is teachable and nobody teaches it.
>
> Let me start where you are: you saw the move on the 19th. I bought it on the 14th, and on the 14th it was an unremarkable stock in a group I did not particularly like.

**结构**：全篇二人称对话，参数出现时说"这个你今晚能自己算"
**收口**：*Redraw the sector map every day for a month. Write to me at the end of it.*

## v10 · 反框架
**轴**：开门否定关于这笔交易的通行叙事。

> Everything you will read about $MRNA this week is wrong in the same way. It will say somebody caught a cancer-vaccine headline, and it will treat the headline as the event. The headline is not the event. The headline is what made a position that already existed suddenly visible.
>
> I was in it five days early, and I want to be precise about what that did and did not require.

**结构**：逐条拆通行说法 —— *"He predicted the data" · "He got lucky" · "He held through it"* 三个都否掉，每否一个给一段真实机制
**收口**：*I did not predict anything. I was standing in the right group with a stop I could afford, which is a much smaller claim and a much more repeatable one.*
