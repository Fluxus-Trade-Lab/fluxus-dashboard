# Round 4 — 开头竞赛（10 版）

*v5 已到 1,518 词，结构和用词都定了。剩下最高杠杆的位置只有两个：**开头**和**收口**。*
*R4 只比开头 —— 同样的事实、同样的用词标准，十种进入方式。*

**判据**：① 冷读者三秒内知道这是什么 ② 不卖关子 ③ Andy 用词六条 ④ 让人想读第二段 ⑤ 不重复标题

---

**v31 · 事实堆（v5 现状）**
> **Aug 14.** I bought $MRNA at 62.72, stop at 60, five percent of the account.
> **Aug 18.** The tape had been rotting for three sessions and the position had gone nowhere for three days, so I cut half, flat.
> **Aug 19, pre-market.** The phase III data hit.
> **Aug 19.** Opened up 84%, closed up **176.97%**. I was out ninety minutes after the open.
>
> Those are the facts. The review has one point: **in the twelve days before that headline, nothing I did right had anything to do with it.**

---

**v32 · 台账开门**
> `$MRNA · in 62.72 Aug 14 · stop 60 · 5% · risk 0.217% · out Aug 19 · +23R`
>
> That line went up before the trade resolved, which is the only reason the rest of this is worth reading. I bought it twelve days before the readout and **nothing I did right had anything to do with the news.**

---

**v33 · 从亏损开门**
> I spent that week losing money in semis.
>
> $MU ran 4% on the 17th and I felt smart for a day; on the 18th it took all of it back, and by then the group hadn't printed a green number all month. That is where the $MRNA trade actually starts — not with a chart I liked, but with a group I owned that wasn't working.

---

**v34 · 会员提问开门**
> Some members asked how I was already inside $MRNA the morning it went 176%. I bought it twelve days earlier and the reasons had nothing to do with the news, so here it is properly instead of five times in the chat.

---

**v35 · 直接给方法**
> **Sector strength picks which one, tightness filters the trade, and risk determines the size.**
>
> That is the whole method, and I am going to show all three on one position — $MRNA, bought August 14 at 62.72, which went up 176% five days later for reasons I did not know about and could not have known about.

---

**v36 · 反问开门**
> Four hundred setups look valid on any Thursday. What decides which one gets your risk?
>
> Not the chart. The chart comes third. On August 14 I bought $MRNA at 62.72 with a stop at 60, and the decision that mattered had already been made two weeks earlier, in a different sector, for reasons that had nothing to do with biotech.

---

**v37 · 时间差开门**
> Twelve days.
>
> That is the gap between the day I bought $MRNA and the day the phase III data made it worth writing about. **Everything I did right happened inside those twelve days, and none of it involved knowing anything about oncology.**

---

**v38 · 承认运气开门**
> The data landing on day five of my hold was luck. I want to get that out of the way first, because everything else in this piece is not luck, and the two get confused constantly.
>
> I bought $MRNA on August 14 at 62.72, stop at 60, five percent.

---

**v39 · 我在睡觉开门**
> It touched nearly 200 in the extended session and I was asleep, because I trade from Asia and I had already been flat for six hours.
>
> That is the least interesting fact about this trade. The interesting one is that I bought it twelve days before anybody had a reason to.

---

**v40 · 事实堆 + 方法句（混合）**
> **Aug 14.** Bought $MRNA at 62.72, stop 60, five percent.
> **Aug 18.** Tape rotting three sessions, position dead three days — cut half, flat.
> **Aug 19 pre-market.** Phase III data hit.
> **Aug 19.** Opened +84%, closed **+176.97%**. Flat ninety minutes after the open.
>
> **Nothing I did right in those twelve days had anything to do with the news**, and you will leave with the three things that did: **sector strength picks which one, tightness filters the trade, and risk determines the size.**
