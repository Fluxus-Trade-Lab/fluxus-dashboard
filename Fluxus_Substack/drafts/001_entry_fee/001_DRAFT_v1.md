## 🔒 墙内

## What changed

**The reading: net advances, 1,431 → −1,408.**

Two supporting numbers from the same five sessions, because one reading is an anecdote:

| | last week | this week |
|---|---:|---:|
| Names up 4% in a day | 642 | **172** |
| Names down 4% in a day | 247 | **852** |
| Share above the 20-day | 50.5% | **41.9%** |

Down-4% names went up by 605. That is a p97 move. Up-4% names fell by 470, p96. Three of the four biggest weekly swings in the whole archive landed in the same five days.

**Why it mattered to me, and not the way you would expect.** A collapse in breadth usually means get smaller. But money leaving and money concentrating look identical in a breadth number, and they are opposite trades. So I looked at where it went instead of how much of it left:

- **Semiconductors Large Caps** — Lagging → **Improving**
- **Cybersecurity** — Weakening → **Leading**
- **Memory & Storage** — Leading → **Improving**
- **Oil & Gas, Energy** — Leading → **Weakening**

Breadth fell apart *and* the leadership got sharper in the same week. That is concentration, not exit.

**So here is what I actually did.**
