3. **给读者一个动作**(「认真看这张图」)
4. **教学稿是他互动最高的类型**(Ultimate Guide 36 赞 / 10 Core Concepts 28 赞 11 评,远高于个股稿)

## 四、标题语法(全档案 356 条的规律)

**他的标题几乎从不是标签,而是一句可证伪的判断或一个画面:**

```
判断句   Trapped Bulls And Trapped Bears · Maximum Pain · Trend Fatigue
         Bear Trap Or The Start Of Something Ugly · Delicate Spot
         The Weekly Close Is What Matters · Block Out The Noise
周期名   The Sentiment Cycle · The Panic Cycle · Disbelief · Wall Of Worry
         Party Like It's '99 · The Most Hated Rally In Years
动作指令 Focusing On The Leaders · The Hunt For Relative Strength
         Situational Awareness Is Key
产品编号 TSF Focus Stocks — 26/7-2026        ← 只有旗舰用日期编号
```

**两条规律:**
- **情绪/周期命名法**(Disbelief, Panic, Sentiment, Wall Of Worry)反复复用 —— **同一个词每隔几个月回来一次,成为可预期的栏目感**
- **旗舰用日期编号,随笔用判断句。** 编号给节奏,判断句给点击
