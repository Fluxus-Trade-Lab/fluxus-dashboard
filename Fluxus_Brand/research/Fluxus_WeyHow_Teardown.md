## 二、⭐⭐ 核心机制:两条帖是一对**互为镜像**的人称

### A. 4 月那条 —— **4,271 字,「you」48 次,「I / my / me」0 次**

`Why Do Some Traders Fail?` / *"They continue to lose, ironically because they are afraid of losing."*

十条编号,**每一条都是「你不敢买」+「你自己的借口」**:

> *"1. You won't buy before BO because you are worried about the resistance… **You said** that's fine you just want to keep it simple…"*
> *"2. Come the BO, but you won't buy the BO because recently all BO seems to fail… **Market awareness & wait for your edge, you said.**"*
> *"3. After a few days past BO you won't buy it too… You are worried that it is already extended…"*
> *"…Anyway there will be another trade right? **That's so you comfort yourself.**"*

「worried / afraid」出现 **12 次**。结尾用别人的话收口(Brian Gelber),不是自己的金句。

> ### 🔑 **这是「说出所有人的疲惫」做到极致的版本 —— 但多了一层:他不只说出疲惫,他把你的借口原样引回来。**
> Za 的版本是「大家都累了」(共情)。他的版本是「**你说你要保持简单 —— 那是借口**」(指认)。
> **零个「我」是关键。整篇没有一句在讲他自己。**

⚠️ **注意他戳的正是我们需求侧测出来的那个卡点:** *"You are worried that it is already extended"* —— **和 U08 的 SNDK 问题、U04 的 "it looks extended… is this a skill gap" 是同一件事。**(见本目录 `Fluxus_Demand_Side_Findings.md` §十)

### B. 7 月置顶 —— **全是「我」**

> *"I am stepping into age 54 this month, and **I nearly quit in 2022 after 20+ years of struggle**. I know how hard and mentally shattering it is."*
> *"only after I nearly quit, my personal pivotal point can really begin"*
> *"My kids are still in their teens, and **they are my motivation and target readers**."*

> ### 🔑 **一篇全「你」(诊断读者),一篇全「我」(交出自己)。两篇都做到收藏比 ~1.0–1.3。**
> **这不是一个日更公式,是一个两篇的结构。** 而且**他没有第三种帖能打** —— 日常帖 1–2K 曝光。
