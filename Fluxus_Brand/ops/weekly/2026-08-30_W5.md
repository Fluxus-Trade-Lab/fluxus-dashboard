### 毛坯 ②（最贴交易的一条 · 直击 how-much 定位 · BUILD/教学）

```
We ran 10,913 breakouts to ask one question: what part of a move is predictable?

Rank the stock's 20-day volatility BEFORE the event. Then measure what happened AFTER.

Size of the move: rho = +0.296.
Direction of the move: rho = -0.006.

Same variable. One column is nearly certain. The other is literally zero.

Probability of a big right-tail move goes from 3.4% in the quietest fifth to 19.0% in the noisiest. Holdout replicated it: 3.4% to 17.5%.

Here's the part nobody wants: it is not a buy signal. The left tail grew with the right tail. Expectancy is negative at both ends.

It's not a signal. It's a divisor. Same dollars into the noisiest bucket = 2.4x the five-day volatility — and that difference was chosen by the screener, not by you.
```

### 毛坯 ⑤（自我审查 · 本线自己的 · BUILD/方法）

```
Our draft claimed: once a stock is 10 ATRs extended, the same risk budget only buys you 44-55% of the position.

Version 1 backed it with two charts: CRM 49.80%, VEEV 50.45%. "Two unrelated names landing on the same answer."

Killed. If your stop sits at the 50-day, "extension" and "stop distance" are the same quantity. Those two didn't independently confirm anything — their extensions were nearly identical (9.68 vs 9.41).

Version 2 backed it with a census: "I ran all 2,091 qualifying names. Not one falls outside 44-55."

Killed harder. The ratio is 0.4(1+10a)/(1+4a). Strictly increasing. On a closed interval its range IS the two endpoints: 44.44 and 54.55. Any stock with a daily range between 2% and 8% is mathematically forced inside that band. There cannot be a counterexample.

Running 2,091 names carried exactly zero information. And the draft invited readers to "break it."

A test that cannot have a counterexample doesn't pass at 100%. It's a tautology wearing a lab coat.

The one real number in the whole thing was the one we nearly threw away: 2,091 of 2,556 tradable names — 81.8% — move between 2% and 8% on an average day.
```
出处：`Fluxus_Brand/ops/campaigns/2026-08-29_extension-arithmetic/RECORD.md`
