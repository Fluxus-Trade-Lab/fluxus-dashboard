## 变体 A · 极简(约 60 字英文,最像你平时的短帖)

```
The headline came after the move, not before it.

$CRM is [9.7] ATR extended. Same stop discipline, and the arithmetic
hands me a position [x]% the size I'd have taken at the base.

I'm not bearish. I'm just smaller.
```

**填:** 只有 `[x]%` 一处 —— 用你的公式在当前止损距离下算出来的相对仓位。
**为什么留它:** 最后一句是整条的钩子,而且**它是主张不是预测** —— 无法被市场证伪,只能被你自己的账本证伪。
⚠️ **风险:** 太短,没有教学价值,收藏比可能低。**它赌的是转发不是收藏。**

## 变体 B · 带自己的时间戳(约 110 字,⭐ 我推荐)

```
Four days ago I wrote that the group being bought wasn't semis — it was software,
and that my book was already there.

Today it's on everyone's screen. That's not confirmation. That's the part of the
move where the arithmetic changes hands.

$CRM [9.7] ATR extended. $VEEV [9.4]. At that distance from a structural stop,
the same 0.25% of risk buys me [x]% of the position it bought at the base.

The chart didn't get worse. The price of being in it did.

[QT 08-24 那篇长文]
```

**填:** `[x]%` 一处。
**为什么推荐:**
- **第一段是收据,不是吹嘘** —— 有公开时间戳,而且用的是「那个组」不是「我抓到了那几只」
- **`the arithmetic changes hands`** 和 **`The price of being in it did`** 是可铸的句子
- **收口回到刊名命题**(how much),给 8/30 的 #001 铺路
- **顺带给 08-24 长文导第二次流**(它还卡在 222 曝光)

⚠️ **纪律:** 别列 PLTR / NOW / ZETA / RBRK 现在赚多少 —— **一晒收益就掉进红海**。
⚠️ **诚实:** 你的四只不是今天涨的四只,**说的是同一个组不是同一批票**。上面的措辞已经用 `the group` 规避了,别改回具体票。

## 变体 C · 指认体微缩(约 120 字,文体 P 的短版试水)

```
You saw $CRM today and you're doing the thing.

You're not asking whether it's a good company. You're asking whether you missed it.
Then you'll put on a normal-size position at [9.7] ATR extended, with the stop
wherever it happens to land, and call it conviction.

Here's the only question that has an answer: at this distance from a structural
stop, what does your risk budget actually buy?

Mine buys [x]% of what it bought at the base. So that's what I own.

I didn't decide to be cautious. I did the division.
```

**填:** `[x]%` 一处。
**为什么值得试:** 这是**文体 P(指认体)的短版第一次试水** —— 全「你」开场,最后一段才转「我」,而且转的是算术不是战绩。
⚠️ **风险最高的一条:** 指认体的纪律是「数字主语必须是你错过」,这里最后两句是「我」。**发之前过一遍音区闸。**
⚠️ 如果读起来有一丝「我做到了而你没有」,**就别发** —— 换变体 B。
