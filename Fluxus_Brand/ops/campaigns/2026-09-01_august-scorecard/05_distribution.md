### V2 · 入口 2 · 架构机制 · 「这张表回答的是我在什么环境开仓」

**hook 类型**：**同名两表钩** ⏳ **首用**（机制定义见 §三）
**载体**：长推

> Every "performance by market condition" table has a hidden timestamp on it, and almost nobody checks which one.
>
> Mine stamps the condition on the day I entered. A trade I opened while my read sat in the bottom bracket, and closed two weeks later when the read was in the top one, is filed in the bottom bracket. All of it — every dollar, all the R.
>
> So my table can tell me what I was opening into. It cannot tell me what I was making money in. Two different tables, same rows, and I only have the first one.
>
> If you build one for yourself, pick the stamp on purpose.
>
> Stamped at entry, it grades the decision to put the trade on.
> Stamped at exit, it grades the tape you closed into.
>
> Neither is wrong. They answer different questions, and a table that doesn't say which stamp it used isn't evidence of anything yet. Ask that of any table you get shown. Mine included.

**独立存在理由**：读者带走的是一条**读表的检查动作**（先找时间戳再读行），可以用在**别人给他看的任何一张归因表**上——与本包其余四条各自的带走物没有交集：V1 给的是结论的门、V3 给的是一个减法、V4 给的是一次拆解、V5 给的是一条自查规矩。**这条是本包唯一在教「怎么读别人的表」的。**

### V3 · 入口 4 · 可复用物（文字形态）· 两步拆法 + 空表

**hook 类型**：**反面先行钩** ⏳（先给会失败的做法，再给规则；首用 `2026-08-29_extension-arithmetic`，**本卡第 2 次**）
**载体**：长推

> Rank your trade types by money and cut the worst one. That's the standard cleanup, and run on my own book it would have had me cut a group that was carrying me.
>
> A money total can only answer one question: how much did this make. A group holding most of your trades ought to make the most money. It would be strange if it didn't. So sorted by money, everything you do is working.
>
> Here's the subtraction that answers the other question. Two steps, and it needs nothing but two columns you already have.
>
> 1. Label every trade by the day you entered it. Any 0–100 read works — a breadth number, your own morning score, honestly even "how good did this look to me, 0 to 10" — as long as the same ruler runs down every row and you never re-score a trade after you know how it ended. Sort the labels, cut into four equal-count groups.
>
> 2. For each group, subtract. Share of your trades, minus share of your total R.
>
> group 1 — trades ___% · R ___% · diff ___
> group 2 — trades ___% · R ___% · diff ___
> group 3 — trades ___% · R ___% · diff ___
> group 4 — trades ___% · R ___% · diff ___
>
> Positive — that group is diluting you. You put in more than it hands back, and the bigger the number the heavier the rate.
> Negative — it's paying you back above its share.
> Around zero — it returns what it takes.
>
> One boundary, and it isn't decoration: any group under ten trades gets no verdict, only its n written next to it. Three of my own four columns fail that test. Write the number. Don't write a conclusion.

**独立存在理由**：**本包唯一一条读者可以直接执行的**——它给的是空表和判据，**不含我账户的任何一个读数**（唯一提到自己的一句是「我自己四列里有三列不达标」，那是把边界用在自己身上，不是战绩）。V1 只承诺有这个工具，V3 才交付它；两条同天发也不冲突。
**出处**：`n<10` 边界与三条判据承 `03_angle.md` §一.6；「三列不达标」＝ n=6/5/3 三档 <10（`05_distribution_derived.txt` §1）。
**CTA**：无（冷读者）。
**数字**：只有 `10`（边界）与 `4`（组数），**都不是读数**。**完全常青**。
**⚠️ 与入口 7 的分界**（`x.md`：同一对象可出 4 也出 7，但两条必须各自独立）：V3 是**文字形态**——三条判据与 `n<10` 边界只能读、不能看。将来 Vera 出图时，图上**不许再放这三条判据**，否则那张图就是 V3 的截图版，不是入口 7。

### V4 · 入口 5 · 批判做法 · 靶子＝「提高胜率」这个工作流

**hook 类型**：**翻译钩** ⏳（把术语读数翻译成人话代价；首用 `2026-08-29_extension-arithmetic`，**本卡第 2 次**）
**载体**：长推
**靶子纪律**：打的是**流行工作流**，⛔ 不点名任何人、不嘲讽对手盘（`x.md` 入口 5 · Swipe_File 音区边界）。

> "Raise your win rate" is the most common advice in this business, and it's advice about a number that isn't really a number about you.
>
> Mine was 40.5% in August. Split the same trades by the read on the day I entered them and that single figure comes apart into four:
>
> 66.7% (n=6) · 0.0% (n=5) · 100.0% (n=3) · 35.7% (n=28)
>
> Same account. Same month. Same person. The 40.5% is just those four averaged together, weighted by how often I was each of them — and 66.67% of the weight sits on the last one. The headline is mostly a report on one group wearing my name.
>
> Which means "raise your win rate" quietly resolves into two entirely different jobs:
>
> — get better inside a group, or
> — change how many trades you put into each group.
>
> One of those is skill. The other is allocation. They are not the same work, and the aggregate number can't tell you which one you owe.
>
> And before anyone runs off with that 100%: it's three trades. The 0% is five. Under ten trades I write the n and no verdict, and so should you.

**独立存在理由**：唯一一条**把总胜率拆成加权平均**的——V1/V3 讲的是 R 的份额，这条讲的是**胜率这个量本身不是一个关于你的量**。读者带走的是一次拆解（我的总胜率是几个人的平均），与 V3 的减法是两个不同动作：V3 问「哪一档在稀释我」，V4 问「我那个数到底是谁的数」。
**出处**（全部 `05_distribution_derived.txt` §1/§3 打印）：`40.5%` 与四档胜率／n 逐格来自月报表；权重 `66.67%` ＝ `28/42`；加权和复算 **40.5%**，与「反推胜数 17/42」**两条独立路径同时给出 40.5%**。
**⛔ n 闸自查（角度站 §六 点名第二处）**：`100.0%` 与 `0.0%` **首次出现即带 `(n=3)`/`(n=5)`**，末段再落一次硬边界。⛔ 全文无任何一处不带 n 的百分比胜率。
**⛔ 环境闸自查**：`the read on the day I entered them` 是**归档动作**；四档在文中只作分组，⛔ 零因果句、零档名（本条连 Damaged/Extended 都没出现，只用 `group`）。
**CTA**：无（冷读者）。

### V5 · 入口 6 · 反馈环 · 我以为那是我给自己加的闸，查完发现那是标准

**hook 类型**：**自拆钩** ⏳（公开拆自己上一说法的漏洞；首用 `2026-08-29_extension-arithmetic`，**本卡第 2 次**）

> I was two paragraphs into a post about a guardrail I thought I'd built, and the fact-check killed it. Posting the dead version instead.
>
> The scoreboard I grade my own system with is Van Tharp's System Quality Number: √N × (mean R ÷ stdev R). The √N is a sample-size term. It means a longer record scores higher than a short one for identical per-trade consistency. Same edge, more rows, better grade.
>
> Mine runs √min(N, 100). The card even prints why: "an uncapped √N inflates a long record into a band it hasn't earned."
>
> I read that as restraint I had imposed on myself and started writing it up that way. Then I went to the source. The cap at 100 is Tharp's own — it's in the standard definition, for exactly the reason my card gives. I hadn't handicapped anything. I'd implemented the metric correctly, which is the baseline, not a virtue.
>
> Two things outlived the post, and both are worth more than it was.
>
> The check is the rule: before you credit yourself with a guardrail, find out whether it's just the standard with your name on it. Mine was.
>
> And the arithmetic matters to anyone who gets quoted an SQN. My closed record is 365 trades. Uncapped, √365 is 1.91× the √100 the capped formula uses — an uncapped SQN on a long record runs at nearly twice the sample-size term of a capped one. A number quoted without its N isn't comparable to anything.
>
> August printed 1.94. "Below average." The cap never even engaged — 42 trades is under 100 either way.

**独立存在理由**：本包唯一一条**内容是「一个被杀掉的说法」**的——落在 NULL 上（BRAIN §一：测了没用的东西如实报）。读者带走的是一条**自查规矩**（把自己的克制拿去和标准对一遍），与 V2 的「读别人的表先找时间戳」不同物：V2 查的是**别人的表**，V5 查的是**自己的功劳**。
**出处**：
- SQN 公式、`1.94`、`Below average`、`Expectancy +1.1R`、`Stdev R 3.83`、以及那句英文原话 `an uncapped √N inflates a long record into a band it hasn't earned` —— 月报自印，本站第一手重读（`05_distribution_derived.txt` §4）。复算 `√min(42,100) × (1.1476/3.83) = 1.9419` → 印出 1.94 ✅
- `365` ＝累计已平仓笔数，**第一手数** `/Users/taolezhu/Documents/AI-Trading-System/data/portfolio/portfolio_2026-08-31.csv` 的 `Closed` 列（`YES 365 / NO 8`，共 373 行）。
- `1.91×` ＝ `√365 / √100`，脚本打印。**这是关于公式的纯比值**，⛔ 不是、也不许被读成「我的累计 SQN」。
- **N=100 封顶是 Tharp 标准口径**：见 §五 的检索留痕与来源链接。
**⛔ 三条硬纪律**：①⛔ 不许写「这道闸让我这个月降了分」——`N=42 < 100`，本月**根本没 binding**，正文已明写；②⛔ 不许把 `1.91×` 乘上 8 月的 mean/stdev 冒充累计 SQN；③ 收口是平的事实句，⛔ 非对仗格言、⛔ 不复述前文主张。
