SQN = √min(N,100) × (mean R ÷ stdev R) — N is capped at 100; an uncapped √N inflates a long record into a band it hasn't earned. Bands: <1.6 poor · 1.6–2.0 below avg · 2.0–2.5 average · 2.5–3.0 good · 3.0–5.0 excellent · 5.0–7.0 superb · ≥7.0 holy grail. Tharp grades a system by the consistency of its R-stream, not by return — raising it means tightening the loss tail, not betting bigger. Same formula as the dashboard's Sizing tab.
