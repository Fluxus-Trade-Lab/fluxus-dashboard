## WHO IS FLUXUS

> Fluxus started as a day trader, got humbled, and grew into a discretionary swing trader — he hunts leaders when momentum is loud and trades ES/NQ futures when it isn't. He learned the trade under a hedge fund manager who had navigated every bear market since the 1980s, which is a long way of saying he learned defense first.
>
> He doesn't treat the market as the world. He treats it as a game — one that takes real-world inputs and then, mostly, ignores them. He holds no strong opinions about where it's going. He holds measurements. The crowd plays on feelings and shows up late; the big funds play on autopilot and sell whatever their risk limits tell them to; his only edge is being the calm one in the room who did the arithmetic before he felt anything. When the odds quietly turn, he's willing to be the first one into the cold water — not because it's brave, but because he already measured it.
>
> The name pays tribute to Fluxus, the 1960s–70s art movement that prized the *event* over the object, chance over control, and dry wit over spectacle. A trade plan, it turns out, is just an event score. Volatility is just chance you've decided to befriend.
>
> *"Insanity is doing the same thing over and over again and expecting different results."*

> **The Algo Monkeys.** The big funds, running on autopilot, forced by their own risk limits to sell weakness and buy strength whether they want to or not.
>
> **You.** The one who waits, measures, and moves when the other two are dumping at once — the calm one who did the arithmetic before feeling anything. The fancy people call this Auction Market Theory. We call it paying attention.
