The first fix was wrong. I tried a three-step lightness ramp and it still failed at ΔE 3.9, because our scale is diverging, not sequential — "took the chance" and "refused" are both strong readings, so no single lightness ramp can hold them apart at the ends and still rank the middle.

What actually works is to stop asking colour to do four jobs: two fills only (#1A5488 / #D1600F, far enough apart to survive greyscale), "partial" as a half-filled cell — area, which Bertin ranks high for ordered data — a 45° hatch on failure as a texture channel immune to any colour condition, and missing data out of the scale entirely, outline with no fill.

Hue now carries only the sign, and nothing carries rank alone. That is the redundant encoding, and below is the measurement.
