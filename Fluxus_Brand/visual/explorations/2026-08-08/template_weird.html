Seven grammars borrowed from seven founders. All professional, none of them ours. Strangeness does not come from ornament — it comes from a category violation performed completely straight, which is exactly why Mr. Update works.

Maciunas coined Fluxus for "flow" and "effluent." The movement's output was scores and instructions, multiples, and work deliberately not finished — MoMA files it under "these artworks aren't finished until you participate." A daily market reading, issued as a numbered, stamped instruction card that the market has to complete, is a category violation the brand name already licenses.

The Bertin test Is hue the right variable for an ordered scale? Measured, not argued.

Bertin ranks the visual variables by what they can carry. For ordered data he puts value (lightness) near the top and hue near the bottom — hue is for categories, not for rank. Our market scale is ordered: took the chance › partial › refused › not tested. So the question is testable: run both encodings through greyscale and through two colour-blindness simulations, and measure whether the steps stay distinguishable.
