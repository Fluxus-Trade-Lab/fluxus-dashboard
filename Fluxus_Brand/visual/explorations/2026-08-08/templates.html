FLUXUS Templates & cast marks ONE VOICE, ONE TEMPLATE

Six templates,
five marks

Every entry in the voice bible that describes how something should be said gets one reusable form here. A template with no matching entry in the bible is decoration and gets deleted; an entry with no template is a voice that only exists in prose.

These are specs, not data views — the slots are shown as slots. Where a filled example appears it uses public market data only. Source: Fluxus_Voice_To_Template.md.

01 · Risk ledger footer Voice bible §3 — "the ledger is the shield made visible"

…the body of any public trade, ending here ↓
TRADE RISK x.xx% PORT RISK y.yy% STOP nn.nn R TARGET n.nR
position fixed footer, always last, always the same four fields size Plex Mono 12px · labels #948F86 · values ink rule every public trade carries it — no exceptions, no "this one is obvious" never never as a headline, never coloured, never with a % sign on the R why an unbroken footer is the only proof the discipline is not selective

02 · Judgment, then execution §4.6 rule 1 — "the blank between them is the product"

What I saw
the condition, as it read at the time — no hindsight, no outcome
—DAYS
What I did
the action, and how long after — or nothing, which is still the answer

gap hatched, labelled in days, NEVER collapsed to "then" rule the number in the middle is computed, never estimated rule if the action was "nothing", the right column says nothing — it does not go blank why the interval is the judgement; deleting it turns a decision into a story

03 · Two losses, never mixed §4.6 rule 3

Probe loss · a cost

Flat, and it may be funny

150 of these at −0.75R is what the method costs to run. Written level. Humour allowed. No mechanism, no apology, no lesson — a probe that fails is the method working.

Judgment loss · an error

Cold, down to the mechanism

One name, one thesis, re-attacked after it broke. Written cold. No humour. Take it apart to the mechanism and say plainly why position discipline could not have saved it.

visual probe = light rule, sits inside the flow judgment = full ink border, stands alone on the page rule the two forms NEVER appear in the same block, and never share a heading rule win rate belongs to probes; judgement belongs to leverage — never the same table why mixing them is the most expensive statistical error in the category

05 · The three X registers §4.7 — 30 manifesto / 40 teaching / 30 joke

Manifesto · 30%
One line. A position, not a prediction.
Full-bleed single line. No image. Never follows a joke.
Teaching · 40%
A number, then the criterion under it.
Template 04. This is the one that earns saves, not likes.
Joke · 30%
Plain text. That is the whole spec.
No layout at all, and never an image. Giving a joke a template is the same mistake as explaining it.
registers X = fragments · the letter = full paragraphs. Never cross the streams. sizes X fragment 15px/1.45 · letter body 18px/1.7 — the difference must be obvious rule a manifesto line may never appear after a joke (it kills the joke) note 30/40/30 is a CONTENT ratio, not a layout ratio

06 · The letter, six parts §6 repeatable format

1 masthead HOW MUCH + date · Plex Cond Bold, letter-spacing .3em 2 headline an aphorism, not a summary · Plex Cond 34px 3 the tape 3–4 cold full sentences: what the Herd does · what the Algo Monkeys are forced to do · the read 4 toggle exactly ONE — art high at tops, grocery low at bottoms. Never both. 5 the trade as measurement: size, stop, R, 1% risk. Defence shown, never bragged. Carries template 01 as its footer. 6 P.S. teaches. **Always warm.** The body is cold and the P.S. is warm — that temperature difference is the retention engine. column 720px · body Plex Sans 18px/1.7 · never fragment lists (reads as AI slop)

Cast marks Each one hangs on a computable quantity — no quantity, no mark

The rule that keeps this honest. A character may only enter a drawing if something in the pipeline can be counted for it. The First Penguin has no public field — his mark needs the trade log, so until that is joined he stays in the prose and off the charts. That is the same discipline as the blank Rates cell.
