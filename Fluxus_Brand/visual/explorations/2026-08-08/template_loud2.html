Set one was one day and one mood. The real question is whether the same five colours survive euphoria, silence and deflation without turning into decoration — because a palette that only works on the day it was designed for is a mood board, not a system.

Three real triggers, none of them 07-31. Same frame, same expression set, same measurement line. What changes is only which colour is doing the work — and, on one of them, whether any colour is allowed at all.

04 · Thrust 2026-06-11 — 705 names up 4%, 36 down

Euphoria drawn as a ratio, not an adjective. 705 against 36 is 19.6 to one. Nothing about that needs a word — the two bars at true scale are the whole statement, and the smaller one is almost invisible, which is the point.

Gold does the work here. It is not "up" — it is the category "the market said yes". On 07-31 the same position was held by indigo and crimson. The palette assigns by what the day is, never by direction.

Holds?
Yes. Gold at this scale is genuinely loud and still not hype, because the loudness is carried by a measured ratio rather than by the colour alone.

05 · Nothing to report 2026-04-27 — 1318 up, 1203 down, net +115

This is the one that tested the palette hardest — by refusing it. Every colour I tried made the card worse. Silence with a gold accent is not silence; it is silence wearing a hat.

So this piece is ink on buff and nothing else. The only colour is the crimson stamp, and it earns its place because it is a stamp on a document rather than a decorative mark.

Holds?
Only by leaving. The finding: "no colour" has to be a legal state of the expression set, not an omission. A system that cannot say nothing will fill every silence, and then it is just a brand that always talks.

06 · Null result 42 sequences tested · 0 beat their baseline

Refusal 5, which has never actually been published. 42 ordered screener sequences were measured against a matched random-entry baseline drawn from the same tickers and the same dates. None of them beat it. The best was −0.41% and the median −2.28% — even the winner loses.

Crimson here is not failure-as-drama. It is the same crimson that marked the dead link on the 07-31 cover: the category "this stopped".

Holds?
Yes, and this is the strongest of the three. Nobody in this category publishes the study that killed their own idea at poster scale. It is the only piece here that could not be made by someone who did not run the study.

What the second set changed

1 · "No colour" is now a state, not an absence. The nothing-to-report card rejected every member of the expression set. That is not a gap in the palette — it is the palette's fifth legal move, and it needs to be written down or the next person will reach for gold to fill it.
2 · Colour assigns by category, never by direction. Gold carried euphoria on 06-11 and crimson carried a stopped current on both 07-31 and the null result. The same crimson meant "the chain died" and "the study died" — same category, different subject. That is the rule working, not a coincidence.
3 · The frame survived three moods unchanged. Same margins, same measurement line, same corner mark. On the tenth piece a reader should recognise the frame before the subject, and after six there is no sign of it bending.
4 · The measurement line is now the weakest part. It held on all six, but it is small and getting smaller as the type grows. On the next set it should be tested at the point where it is genuinely inconvenient — otherwise it will quietly become a footnote nobody reads, which is the same as not measuring.
