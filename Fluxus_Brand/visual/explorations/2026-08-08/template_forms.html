Three forms from Fluxus_Work_Forms.md, built with data that already exists. No new pipeline, no new fetch. The one thing that did need building was a judgment function: "did anything actually change today?" — because a refusal you cannot verify is a pose.

Measured first How often is the silence real?

01 · The rules, as the work After Sol LeWitt — the buyer receives a certificate and instructions

Why this form. LeWitt sold a certificate and a set of instructions; other people executed the drawing. "The idea becomes a machine that makes the art."

That is literally what the verdict engine is: twelve rules, one threshold table, and the conclusion falls out. Publishing the table as the work says the thing no financial account ever says — here is the machine, run it yourself, you do not have to believe me.

It is also the cheapest possible proof of honesty. Anyone who runs it and gets a different answer has caught you.

02 · A receipt for nothing After Yves Klein — he sold a receipt, and the buyer burned it

Why this form. Klein sold "zones of immaterial pictorial sensibility": the buyer paid in gold, received a receipt, and burned the receipt. A document certifying that nothing was received.

This one is dated —, the most recent session where the judgment function says nothing moved. It is not a mood — it is the output of a rule, and the rule fires about — of sessions.

The catch I hit building it: the first version called 2026-07-29 a still day. That session had 637 names down 4%. Bucketed levels had all held, so nothing "changed" — bucketing eats magnitude. A raw-move guard now sits beside the levels, and 07-29 is correctly rejected. Without that guard this card would have gone out on a liquidation day, which would have destroyed the whole device.

03 · The institution After Broodthaers — he invented a museum and gave it stationery

In 1968 Broodthaers declared a fictional museum — Musée d'Art Moderne, Département des Aigles — and the work was its apparatus: letterhead, seals, departments, crates, an opening. Institutional furniture as the medium.

These are the reusable elements. Unlike the cards above, these are allowed on the dashboard — a stamp and a file number, never a whole document.
