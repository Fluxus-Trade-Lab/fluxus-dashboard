What stays true at every level

1 · The measurement line. Every piece above still ends with the day's real numbers in mono. A cover that stops measuring is decoration with a logo on it.
2 · The expression set is quarantined. Crimson, gold, moss, indigo and ochre may never appear on the dashboard. One breach and the blue/orange stop meaning anything.
3 · Hue never carries rank. Here it carries category and feeling. The moment a colour starts meaning "more" or "less", it goes back under the instrument rules and gets measured.
4 · Occasional means triggered. These run on the poster calendar's triggers — extremes, a judgement paying off, a month boundary — capped at three a week. Loud every day is not loud.
