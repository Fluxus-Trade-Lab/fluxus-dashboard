Silver has one member. A theme of one stock is one stock, so the count is printed in the same weight as the name and turns amber below three. 16 of the 80 themes have fewer than three members — they are kept, and marked, rather than quietly dropped or quietly averaged in.

What this page refuses to do. It does not rank themes by what they returned. A list sorted by past gain puts a name at the top precisely because it already moved, which is a selection, not a finding — and it is the reason our own confluence study came back 0 of 42. Level and acceleration are both measured against SPY over fixed windows and neither is chosen after the fact.

What it still cannot tell you. Whether Improving becomes Leading more often than it falls back to Lagging. The four states are computable today; the transition rates are not measured yet, so no claim is made about them.
