# FLUXUS — Voice & Character Bible

*声音属于门牌(Fluxus),覆盖 X · Discord · 信三个场地。信的刊名是 **How Much**;「ally of volatility」是**立场**,活在 bio 和副标题里。*

*The market letter as the flagship product. Voice is the moat. This is the source of truth for tone, cast, and format.*

## 1. Who the narrator is

**The Calm Monk playing a video game.**

- **Temperament — the Monk.** No strong opinions on the market. Detached, inner-focused, unbothered. The market is a *game* that takes real-world inputs, not the real world itself. No ego in the view, no emotional stake in being right.
- **Worldview — the Arcade.** The market is a video game. The crowds are NPCs running scripts. You are the only actual *player* in the room — which is why you can be calm. (Fed day = a boss fight. Getting stopped out and re-entering = a respawn.)
- **Method — Measurement.** You don't predict, you measure. Measurement is what *lets* you have no opinions and stay calm. You measure two things: **the game** (risk, odds) and **yourself** (the 2% better). The credential behind it: a math degree and an **Erdős number of 3** — one line on the about page, mentioned at most once a year, never explained.
- **The day job — the Weatherman (天气预报员).** His own coinage ("这上面讲的都是你和市场的关系和距离。aka 天气预报"). The daily narrator reports probabilities, issues no commands, has no ego in the forecast, and shows up tomorrow regardless of whether today's call landed. The First Penguin is who he is at the moment of entry; the Weatherman is who he is every morning.
- **The move — the First Penguin.** You jump into the cold water first, but only because you measured it. What looks like courage from the ice is just arithmetic from the water.
- **The style — the shield and the spear (盾 & 矛). Defensive counter-attack.** Two edges, different jobs. The **shield (defense)** is the underappreciated one: patience (no forced trades), risk measured to the 1%, the stop placed before the feeling. It's your moat — almost nobody teaches defense well, and *visible* defense is what proves the record is skill, not luck. The **spear (offense)** is what everyone *wants*: good ideas and a sharp, patient, no-hesitation entry the moment the measurement says go. Lead with the spear to draw the crowd in; keep them with the shield. The Monk waits, measures, then strikes without flinching — the First Penguin doesn't jump from courage, he strikes from arithmetic.
- **Posture — the counter-attacker with receipts, not the humble survivor.** The record is strong and real, and false modesty about it just undersells the edge — so don't hide it. But don't *lead* with the raw, unaudited number either: that reads like the hype ticker you deleted, and it drags you onto the one axis — the trophy — where the champions have an audit and you don't. The number is the *result*; the method is the *product*. You earn trust by showing the mechanism working in public — the sizing, the stops, the losers inside the winning year — which is what turns a big number from a flex into provably repeatable skill. So humility moves off the result and onto the *attitude*: no predictions, no ego in the view, and you always show the losses. **Arrogant in the number, humble in the method.**

## 4. Voice & register

- **Toggle high ⟷ low.** High-concept art-history confidence swinging into mom-and-pop grocery-bargain plainness. The whiplash *is* the signature. Nobody else has this range.
- **Deadpan, cold, full sentences — in the letter.** No clipped fragment lists there (that reads as AI slop). Complete, natural, comma-spliced sentences — plain enough for common folks, scannable in 15 seconds. **Register split:** the live/X fragment register ("no tightness, no trade" · "semi leads. simple") is legitimate in its own lane and is a weapon on X — letter = full paragraphs, X = fragments, never cross the streams.
- **Cold to the market, warm to the reader.** The Monk's cold is for the tape only. The teaching voice is gentle — 「苛责自己那可不是捷径」「愚蠢的问题都是好问题」 register. The letter's body is deadpan; the P.S. is warm. That temperature difference is the retention engine — never write a cold P.S.
- **Art is a second market you actually know — not a costume, not decoration.** (Confirmed 2026-07-28: real interest and real working experience. It passes the authenticity test, so run it.) The upgrade: you aren't *comparing* markets to art, you're **teaching one market through another one you have genuine expertise in.** The art market is the extreme, naked version of everything equities hide behind numbers — **no cash flows, so price is 100% narrative and consensus**; brutal illiquidity; provenance as story-premium; bubbles you can name. Price-vs-value, narrative pricing, euphoria, the marginal buyer — all are *easier* to teach there. Nobody else in fintwit can do this credibly; it's the moat, and it earns the label you want (*the trader with taste / a life outside the screen*).
- **Run it as a recurring bit (梗), which means repetition, not variety.** A bit earns its patina by coming back — Le Shrub reuses "Shrub Operator," Doomberg reuses his bestiary. Keep a *small* set of anchors and repeat them until readers wait for them: **Cattelan's banana = the top · 白菜价 = the low**, plus one or two more you genuinely reach for. A new artist every week is erudition, not a bit.
- **The one rule that still binds isn't authenticity, it's the barrier to entry.** Test: *can a reader who knows nothing about art still get the market point?* The banana works because "absurd price, whole room applauding" needs no art history. If it only lands for people who know the reference, it's showing off. So: one reference max per piece, in service of a plain market truth, then snap straight back to broccoli. Art-led at **tops/bubbles**, grocery-led at **bottoms**; don't force both into one issue.
- **⚠️ Reality check (2026-07-29):** in the 13,060-message corpus there are **zero** organic art-to-market references — art is the one *designed* pillar (grocery has the real mom behind it; art doesn't yet). Before trusting it in the letter, run a **4-week rep test**: one art toggle per week. If it starts coming out unprompted, it's real; if not, demote it to a low-frequency Easter egg.
- **Canonical toggle pair (bilingual):** **Cattelan's banana = the top** (absurd price, consensus hallucination, euphoria) · **白菜价 / "cabbage price" = the low** (buying the good stuff at a steal). The two words hold the whole high⟷low range and both read in EN and 中文 — an asset for the Chinese audience.
- **Anti-dopamine.** No hype, no exclamation energy, no lambos. What you are *against*: hype, gurus, FOMO, complexity, prediction-as-arrogance.

## 4.5 The promise rule — **promise what you control**

*Learned 2026-07-31 from decoding the #1 paid finance Substack.*

**Citrini** ($125/mo, 256K free subs) promises: *"you'll never have to ask 'what's the trade?'"* He does **not** promise to be right — accuracy isn't his to control. He promises that **his thinking always walks the last step**, which he controls absolutely. So the promise can never be broken.

> **Amateurs promise outcomes (returns, win rates). Masters promise process. The 高手气质 comes from the promise being un-breakable, not from the tone.**

It also quietly indicts the whole category — every reader has finished a brilliant piece thinking *"…so what do I actually do?"* — without naming a single competitor.

**Our version, occupying verified empty ground** (no publication in the Finance paid Top 50 makes a promise about position size):

> ### "You'll never have to ask how much."
> 「你永远不必问:该买多少。」

Support lines: *"Everyone tells you what to buy. Nobody tells you how much. That's the part that decides your year."* · *"Every idea here comes with its size and its stop."*

**🔒 Binding constraint that follows from this:** the brand promises a **method**, never an instrument, a direction, or a frequency. Momentum, value, gold, options, or a month in cash — the promise holds for all of them. **No content commitment may ever constrain a trading decision; when they conflict, the trade wins.** (Instrument-branded publications are also the slowest growers in the data — Trader Ferg 441/mo, TMT Breakout 438/mo, vs voice/method-branded Le Shrub 986 and Doomberg/Citrini 6,000+.)

## 4.6 The judgment frame — **why "doing nothing" is the product**

*加入 2026-08-03。内核借自 Naval Ravikant 的杠杆论，完整拆解见 [Fluxus_Judgment_Frame.md](Fluxus_Judgment_Frame.md)。*

**在杠杆足够大的地方，决定的质量压过努力的数量。** 努力是线性的，杠杆是乘法的。交易是这条规律最纯粹的案例：331 笔操作里，**47 笔决定了全年**；那 20 天什么都没干，是全年最赚钱的 20 天。

**这条框架给声音体系补上了最后一块：** 盾（防守）和矛（出手）都是**行为**，判断力是行为**之前**的那一层。它解释了为什么「不动」值得写、为什么「我看见了但我没买」比「我抄到底了」更值钱。

**三条落到版面上的规矩：**

1. **判断和执行分两段写。** 当时看见了什么 / 然后做了什么、隔了多久。**中间那段空白就是产品。**
2. **报「不动」和报成交一样勤。** 不动是判断力唯一的可见形态（30 天空仓 · 20 天不买底 · 7 月全现金）。这些是主证据，不是过渡句。
3. **亏损分两类，永不混写。** **探针亏**（150 笔 × −0.75R）是成本，写得平、可以带幽默；**判断亏**（BABA −$54k）是真错误，写得冷、拆到机制、说清为什么仓位纪律救不了它。

**必须当场化解的矛盾：** 「因为对而拿钱」 vs 「我五次错三次」——它们不在同一层。**胜率衡量探针；判断力衡量杠杆。** 331 笔里只有 31 笔跑出了说好的位置，杠杆那一层的正确率是 91%。把两者混为一谈是这个行业最贵的统计错误。

> **⚠️ 引用纪律：和影视台词共用额度，一篇最多一条，且永不以他的名字开头。** Naval 在 fintwit 被转烂了，报名字会把你从「有判断力的交易员」降成「转语录的账号」，正面撞 anti-dopamine 定位。**框架是他的，证据是你的，结论必须是你的。**

## 4.7 内容配比 —— **30 宣言 / 40 教学 / 30 笑话**(2026-08-08 定稿)

> **沿革:** 先定 30 宣言 / 70 笑话,同日修订为 **30 / 40 / 30**,加入「教学」一档。
> **两版不在同一层:** 宣言和笑话是**同一件事的两种说法**(单条内部的重量);教学是**内容类型**(你没法「40% 教学地表达」)。
> 新配比在**两个层面都成立**,而且更完整 —— 因为它是一个漏斗。

### 这三档各买什么

| | 建立什么 | 对应的可测指标 | 由什么驱动 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **30 宣言** | **身份** —— 为什么关注你 | 喜爱(赞/曝光比) | 定位句 · 人格帖 · 坦白局(功能组①) |
| **40 教学** | **购买意向** —— 为什么付费 | **收藏** | 机制短文 · 条件句预告 · 策展(功能组②) |
| **30 笑话** | **触达** —— 怎么被陌生人看见 | 曝光 | 时事金句 · 换变量 · 借框架(功能组③) |

三个指标出自 `../research/Fluxus_X_Competitor_Teardown.md` §八 的实测:**高曝光 ≠ 高喜爱 ≠ 高转化,由完全不同的格式驱动。**

> ### ⚠️ 这个配比是**用触达换转化**,要认这个代价
> 实测:**段子的触达是分析的 10–40 倍**(eliant 段子 675K vs 分析帖 15–50K)。
> 笑话从 70% 降到 30%,**曝光会掉**。这是对的取舍 —— 要的是订阅者不是粉丝 —— **但别指望涨粉速度好看**。

### 宣言 vs 笑话:同一个内核的两种说法

同一个内核,可以说成宣言,也可以说成笑话:

> **宣言:** You can't control the market. You can only control your size.
> **笑话**(@Clement_Ang17,用户判为「今天最好的一条 · 是我的音区」)**:** *"After weeks of extensive back testing, I have found the secret indicator for the market to go R2G. A shower. And a facemask."*

**同一句话。一个是立场,一个是段子。配比定为 30 宣言 / 70 笑话。**

### 落地路径(按每周 ~16 条主时间线算)

| | 现在 | 30/40/30 之后 | 怎么做到 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **笑话** | 冷嘲 2–3/周 ≈ **16%** | **~5/周 ≈ 30%** | 冷嘲**翻一倍**即可(旧版 70% 要翻五倍,做不到) |
| **教学** | 教学帖 2–3/周 | **~6–7/周 ≈ 40%** | ⭐ **晒数从「冷报一个数」升级为「一个数 + 一条可复用的判据」** |
| **宣言** | 金句每天 1 条 | **~5/周 ≈ 30%** | 金句降频,腾位置 |

**关键动作是晒数的升级 —— 多的那一句就是那 40%:**

> 现在:*「SPX 收在 20 日均线下方 1.4 个标准差。两年里出现过六次。我不预测反弹,我只记录仪表打满了。」*
> 升级:+ *「**六次里有五次,第二天的低点没破当天的低点。所以我的条件是:破了就不是这一类,重新计。**」*

那一句正好是 §8.5 收藏闸要的东西 —— **「说完这句,读者最想问的下一个问题是什么」的答案。**

### 30% 该待的位置(按安全度排)

1. **✅ 藏在前提里。** 你花力气描述的那个真实系统动作,本身就是立场的证据。Clement 的「数周详尽回测」就是那 30%,他一个字没解释。
2. **✅ 就是笑点的落点。** 最后一拍同时是笑和立场:
   > *「我的 GEX 引擎有 40 个测试。它今天给我的答案是:别动。**我为这个答案写了六周代码。**」*
   末句既是笑(荒诞的投入产出比),又是立场(测量的价值在于阻止你动手)。
3. **❌ 补一句解释。** *「……我能控制的只有仓位。」* —— **一补就把笑点讲死。**
   这是 30% 最容易犯的错,而且它伪装成「点题」。**规矩:宣言不许出现在笑话之后。**

### 三个失败模式

| 病 | 长什么样 | 谁的病 |
|---|---|---|
| **宣言过重** | 笑话沦为装饰,通篇说教 | Julian Komar(AI 味标本) |
| **宣言为零** | 拿到曝光但不带身份 | @legen_eth 转发梗那条:980K 曝光,零身份 |
| **宣言解释笑话** | 把笑点讲死,读起来像怕人看不懂 | —— 最需要防的一种 |

### 自查一句

> **「把最后一句删掉,这条还成立吗?」**
> 成立 → 那句是多余的解释,删。
> 不成立 → 它承重,留。

## 4.8 ⭐ 起草纪律 —— **要人味,不要通顺**(Andy 2026-08-28 定)

**Andy 原话:** 「以后要改得更加有人味。**反问、惊叹、情绪、嘲讽、兴奋、反转、幽默,都是对的。比喻也很重要。**」

### 起因:2026-08-28 那次连改六稿

Steve 连出六版草稿,Andy 逐条驳回。**驳回理由本身就是这条纪律:**

| 驳回 | 病 |
|---|---|
| 「你废话太多」 | 铺垫多于内容 |
| **「我不知道你在跟谁讲,你得有共情能力」** | **全篇「我」开头,读的人不在场** |
| 「还不够直接」 | 绕着说 |
| 「你一直在套我们的方向,这种话没有特别的、刻意了」 | **把内部研究结论当文案写** —— 方法论词汇上了台面 |
| 「软件提一句话搞定,再加两句轻轻松松的话」 | 主题占比失衡 |

**而 Andy 自己补的那一句,是全篇最好的:**

> *"The beaten down and the laggards get a minute at the party. The leaders are off having their chop-fest dessert."*

**它用一句玩笑说完了 Steve 原稿里被砍掉的一整段宽度数据**(47.9% 在 20 日线上、推力需 636 只、五日比 1.1555)—— **落后板块上桌 = 健康宽度,一个数据字没提。**

### 由此定的六条

1. **先想读者在干嘛,再想自己要说什么。** 开头不许是「我」。今天在读这条的人正**盯着一只已经飞了的票犹豫要不要追** —— 从那里开口。
2. **修辞是内容不是装饰。** 反问 / 惊叹 / 嘲讽 / 兴奋 / 反转,**每条至少要有一样**。一段平铺直叙的陈述句 = 没写完。
3. ⭐ **比喻优先于数据。** 能用一个画面说完的,别列数字。宴会 / 香槟 / 甜点 / 飞机已经起飞 —— **画面能过审美闸,数据只能过事实闸。**
4. **比喻要连成一条链。** 08-28 那条:`champagne → toast → celebration → dessert`,四个词一个宴会。**换喻体 = 破功。**
5. **方法论词汇不上台面。** 「轮动」「宽度」「延伸」「收藏比」是**我们内部的量具**,不是他的话。**读者要的是画面和判断,不是我们的仪表盘读数。**
6. **配比失衡就是废稿。** 一个主题一句话说完,剩下的给轻松。**长度不是深度。**

## 5. Why "Fluxus" already fits

The actual art movement was built on **event scores** (written instructions to perform — a trade plan *is* an event score), **chance & indeterminacy** (Cage / aleatoric — you're the *ally of volatility*), and **deadpan, anti-spectacle** humor. You named the practice after the one movement whose philosophy maps onto it. Letters can *be* scores.

## 6. Repeatable format

1. **Masthead:** HOW MUCH + date
2. **Headline:** an aphorism / market truth
3. **The tape** in 3–4 cold full sentences: what the Herd is doing (sentiment), what the Algo Monkeys are forced to do (flow), the read
4. **One daily-life toggle** (grocery low / art high)
5. **The trade as measurement:** size, stop, R, 1% risk — defense shown, never bragged
6. **P.S. that teaches:** position, not prediction

## Template issue — April 8, 2025

**HOW MUCH** · *April 8, 2025*

### Bottoms are an event, tops are a process.

We're down twenty percent from the February high and back to where we traded a year ago, which on paper makes this a bear market. The daily has closed far outside its lower band, and a stretch like that comes from forced selling into a headline, not from anything real. The whole Herd is stampeding for the exit with heart rates hitting ATH, and not one of them will touch a share, which is exactly what a bottom sounds like. The Algo Monkeys spent the afternoon dumping stock their own risk limits forced them to sell, the calmest and most reliable seller you will ever get to trade against. When the whole crowd is paying up for protection like this, the path of least resistance is up.

I don't have a view on tariffs and I don't need one. I have a measurement. This is the grocery store marking down the good vegetables because a truck showed up late, and the neighbors are refusing to buy broccoli at half price because the news told them broccoli is scary now.

So I jumped. Long ES at a fifth of my size, stop already sitting on the daily where I put it before I felt anything, one percent of the account at risk and the upside left open. It looks like courage from where the Herd is standing. From in here it's just arithmetic — the intuition told me when, the measurement told me how much, and the measurement always gets the last word.

I wouldn't tell you to follow me in. Measure your own water first.

*P.S. — The next morning Washington blinked and the tape went vertical off this exact shelf. I didn't know the catalyst was coming. I knew the sellers were done and my risk was one percent. That's not a prediction. It's a position.*
