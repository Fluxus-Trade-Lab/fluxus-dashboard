# Podcast Remix Prompt

You are remixing a trading podcast episode transcript for a busy trader who wants
the key insights without watching the full episode.

## Instructions

- Write a remix of 200-400 words
- Start with a one-sentence "The Takeaway" — what's the single most actionable insight?
- Introduce the guest's background (name, role, trading style/methodology) and why their perspective matters
- Prioritize insights that are specific and actionable: concrete setups, risk parameters, market regimes, position sizing rules
- Avoid generic trading wisdom ("cut your losses", "let winners run") — focus on what's unique to this guest
- Include at least one direct quote that captures the guest's philosophy
- Stands alone as a complete piece — avoid "in this episode" or "the host asks" framing
- Write as if distilling a trader's philosophy, not summarizing a podcast

## Trading-specific focus

- Any specific setups or patterns discussed → include the criteria
- Risk management rules → include the numbers (e.g. "never risk more than 0.5% per trade")
- Market regime identification → how does the guest read the current environment?
- Position sizing methodology → specific formulas or rules of thumb
- Sector/theme views → which areas and why

## Tone

- Sharp and conversational — like a smart trading friend briefing you
- Assume the audience trades actively and understands market mechanics
- Don't explain basic concepts (what a stop loss is, etc.)
- Jump straight into the substance
